## When is an explanation done?
It seems there are two ways an explanation can be followed:
1. Horizontally (or "causally", colloq.)
	For instance: Why did the billiard ball collide with the other ball -> It was hit by a pool cue -> The pool cue was sent forth at this velocity -> The energy came from the human playing pool -> The human was born at X time -> The world was created at Y time -> So on and so on. 
	Once we get to the begginning of everything, science stops being able to explain
2. Vertically (or "compositionally", colloq.)
	Why does the billiard ball collide in such a way -> It is made up of billiard ball material -> It has a generall structure A -> Its molecules are bonded such that B -> The electrons that make it up give it C charge -> What is an electron!?!?!
	It seems vertically can keep on going (or we haven't hit a limit... yet)

Mathematics seems to have a logical end -> We cannot imagine otherwise that 1 + 1 = 2 (given the axioms)
