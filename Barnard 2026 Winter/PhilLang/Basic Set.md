#language #philosophy 

The set of words that vary ([[Context Sensitivity]]) in terms of truth value, reference, what is said, etc.
E.g. today, now, tomorrow, here, there, that, we, she, he, us, it, ...

Though what is said when 'I' is uttered varies depending on context, the stability of its meaning (the speaker) remains unchanged 

THeir status as context sensitive is not up for debate
It is debated which words exactly are a part of the basic set
