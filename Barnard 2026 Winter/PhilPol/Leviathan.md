#philosophy #politics 

A work of [[Political Philosophy]]
Wrote on the [[State of Nature]], describing it to be "nasty, brutish, solitary, depressing, and short." Argued that the best form of government is one where some individual or single group contains absolute power (that goes unquestioned by the people) in order to maintain a guarantee of security. Makes sense for Hobbes, as he was a royalist during the British civil war.
Notably argued about LIBERTY ([[Freedom]]) not as a freedom from independence, but rather a freedom from any external constraints. This has influenced a lot of liberal thinking on the matter (as is evident with the U.S. - we have become dependent on all Government)

*Natural rights* - the rights for any man to acheive what he so desires, which is inherently in conflict with the EQUALITY present in the state of nature. Thus, man must be in eternal WARRE with his fellow man, and it is the powerful who triumph over the weak.
**Natural laws**
1. Fundemental Law - That a man in the state of nature must seek PEACE
2. Secondary Law - That man should give up his right to all things and enjoy the LIBERTY of his only so much as he could bear to have others enjoy their liberty infringing against him ([[Golden Rule (and shù, 恕)]])
3. Third Law - That man can make contracts, giving up rights to seek security, and must fulfil such contracts
--- 
4. Fourth Law - That man cannot go against his own WILL, DESIRES, etc. - and thus must accept a Gift or Grace. The law of GRATITUDE
5. Fifth Law - COMPLEASANCE, every man strive to accomodate himself to the rest - and thus be sociable, even if it doesn't immediatly help you, society emerges from this quality
6. Sixth Law - That upon caution of the Future time, a man ought to pardon the offences past of them that repenting, desire it - PARDON and grant peace
7. Seventh Law - That Revenge or retribution should look ahead towards greater goods and not greater, past evils. Retribution must be net positive
8. Eight Law - against CONTUMELY, or do not hate/contempt fellow man so that retribution doesn't arise
9. Ninth Law - PRIDE - rulers are placed by consent of men, as all are equall and every man should acknowledge such
10. Tenth Law - Against ARROGANCE, in pursuing peace we shouldn't linger onto any rights we don't want other men to have, or be MODEST
11. Eleventh Law - Deal equally between men, or EQUITY
12. Twelth Law - What can't be divided must be common
13. Thirteenth Law - Distribution, if it can't be equal or divided, should be determined by LOT
14. Fourteenth Law - The first posessor should lay claim to such things in 13
15. Fifteenth Law - All men be allowed safe conduct if they contribute to peace
16. Sixteenth Law -Submit to an ARBITRATOR (judge) if there is confusion about fulfilling a covenant
17. Seventeenth Law- No man is his own ARBITRATOR
18. Eighteenth Law - Partial people shouldn't be judges
19. Nineteenth law - Witnesses are needed in the event of questions "of Fact" for judges to make a decissioon
And so on... "Do not that to another, which thou wouldest not have done to thy selfe"
***Contract*** - A bidirectional exchange of natural rights between a number of individuals. (If it is unidirectional, it is a GIFT, or otherwise GRACE of God). Contracts, if not enforced by a higher body (government) must be enforced by the greatest power: God, as a contract is completed one side at a time, and there is no guarentee that the other person will complete their end of the bargain if there is no TRUST. People must make contracts under the fear of God for them to be valid. If a contract cannot be made, then WARRE can be reentered in order to enforce one's power over another
	1. Expresse Signs - Explicit speech declarationns that constitute a contract; e.g. "I will that I grant you these rights"
	2. Implicit Signs - Implicit actions that constitute a contract; e.g. coercion, force, gestures, etc. Hobbes argues that real contracts are made with expresse signs, and that "beasts" cannot create contracts for the reason that they cannot translate what is at stake.
	A COVENANT/PACT is a contract met only by one party. A covenant cannot reasonably be unjustly fulfilled.
Interestingly, Hobbes argues that a man can never make a contract that goes against the fundemental law of nature (that guarentee's man's death), as a dead man can't will his own rights away, and thus we expect men to fight back if they are sentenced to death, even if they try to go against a contract and make it NULL.

*Justice* - Fulfilling a contract according to its terms
**Injustice** - Rejecting/breaking a contract

A just person is not defined by a singular just act. A just act appears both as 'commulative', or the fulfillment of contract, and 'distributive' or laying out the terms of a contract. Both combine together to create the virtue of justice
