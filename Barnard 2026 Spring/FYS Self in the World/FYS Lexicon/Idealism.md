#philosophy 

The belief that everything that exists are ideas that are perceived, necessarily, by a [[Mind (Nous)]]/[[Spirit (Geist)]]. This implies that either there exists more than one mind, to explain continuity of the external world, or that mind is fundemental [[Substance (Arche)]] of all things. The belief in matter is trumped by the recognition that reality, existence, is equivalent to an idea being perceived, being conscious, and thinking. This aids in reconciling the mind-body problem and the problem of individual things existing from one substance.
This combats the Platonic/Skeptic ([[Skepticism]]) theory of a veil of perception, where we are locked behind seeing the true nature of things because of our senses. How can we possibly submit to the idea that there exist empirical things that will never be perceived by the senses? It is just as plausible that they do not exist.
