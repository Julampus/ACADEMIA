#philosophy 

A "second sight," so to speak, whereby the object of observation can see the illusory veil through which they are seen, as well as the subject which is observing the veil (and not the object in its true form). Thus, out of a need for survival, one develops a double consciousness of not only how they perceive themselves ([[Ego]]), but how the world around them (more specifically, the oppressors and the world they have crafted around them) perceives them.

[[W.D.B. DuBois]] coined this term for the African American experience under institutionalized racism, though it is just as applicable to the field of transsexual experience.
