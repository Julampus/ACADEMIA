#philosophy 
See: [[Michel Foucault]]

A structured way of thought, belief, and speech that shape knowledge, power, and social reality - the synthesis of knowledge and "[[Truth]]" via. language (all practices have a discursive aspect) - thus, [[Representation]]al meaning is found in all social practices
As discourse is constructed, so too is [[Ignorance]] manufactured

More about speech than values (as with [[Ideology]])

Discourse derives its meaning from
	Statements on concepts that provide context and definitions
	Subjects to discursive concepts and the institutional practices that they are subject to
	An understanding of the specific discourse's relation to power
	The rules (linguistic/cultural) which the discourse follows
	The potentiality of a new discourse supplanting the current one (a state of discursive precarity)

E.g. "Gender Identity" is not a material fact, but rather an instantiation of a discourse surrounding sexed bodies and individual expression that is organized and informed by history, institutions, and the linguistic rules of gender.
Or, the discourse on sex as repressed obscures the fact that we are, in reality, obsessed with sex
