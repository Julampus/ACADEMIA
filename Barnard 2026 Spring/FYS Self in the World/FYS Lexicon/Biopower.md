#philosophy 

The control in which states exert over populations, over bodies as a collective
	This control is often achieved through the use of [[Institution]] ([[Ideological State Apparatus (ISA)]])

In the past, the absolute monarch and the serfs had a direct relationship of rule - the rule over bodies, the power to declare death and end life
Nowadays, the state sees life as a commodity, as power in and of itself. The state has granted itself thus not power over death, but power over life - which life to permit and use and which life to spend and end
