#philosophy 
## Marxism
A Marxist ([[Karl Marx]]) philosophy - that there exist no perfect things (nor can there possibly be perfect things) and all that there is is what is (as opposed to [[Idealism]])

*Dialectical Materialism* - That change exists because there is difference in the world. A [[Dialectic]] occurs in which one of the things wins out and creates a synthetic change

**Historical Materialism** - That "the history of all hitherto existing societies is the history of class struggles." History ought to be measured in economic and technological advancements
