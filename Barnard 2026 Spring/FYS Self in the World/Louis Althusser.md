## Ideology and Ideological State Apparatuses 
Ideology - A set of beliefs attributed to a group/person
Subject - One that lives in ideology

People are all (ideological) subjects - *ideological animals* - they exist within the context of a given ideology
	Individuals are an abstraction of one's subjectivities
	One cannot be isolated from ideology and socialization... there doesn't exist a concrete individual
	As we are named as individuals, and that that is "obvious," too is an ideological effect
Ideology imposes obviousness as obviousness (in a hidden sense) - *ideological misrecognition function*
	In other words, we are told to believe something is obvious (when ideology makes it so)
	and its inverse...
**Ideological Misrecognition Function** - that that which is not obvious is not subject
	We are told to believe something is in obscurity (when ideology makes it so)
Ideology exists in everything we do (our beliefs) - most often appear in the rituals we perform
We are subjects by recognition - but how does this mechanism work? Through ideology
	Ideology recruits individuals -> Subjects by hailing
	
Ideology existing = Individuals -> Subjects (one is the result, one is the mechanism)

[[Ideological State Apparatus (ISA)]] are used to enforce an ideology to certain subjects (e.g. Medical Gendering, Police Force, National Monuments, etc.)
