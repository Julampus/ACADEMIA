#linguistics 

Furthered research into [[Semiotics]], a spiritual successor to [[Ferdinand de Saussure]]

Notably, Barthes claimed that signs are not just limited to the system of spoken/written language, but also symbol (art, fashion, culture, ritual, etc.)
	We can say the physical structure and formation are indicative of the primary level of *denotation* of object as sign, while the cultural meaning of such material and form describe the secondary **connotation** of sign.
	This **connotation** is directly informed by and constitutes [[Ideology]] - Barthes called this the level of "**myth**" (what are they trying to illustrate - is this a faithful description/imitation or, more likely, ideological [[Representation]])
