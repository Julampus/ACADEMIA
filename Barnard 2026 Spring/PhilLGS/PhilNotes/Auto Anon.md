Wrote *Reverse Tomboy*

A story of embracing (or coming to terms with) masculinity and transfeminism, of problematic HRT gaps and lost loves. We are able to move on from our traumas, we prove ourselves wrong, if not through rigorous work then merely through the passage of time, and we find a light that pulls us out into a world slightly better than what we've known before. It's hard out there, people expect you to be a caricature and then degrade you being just that - butch identity and trans lesbians aren't a category that most people can comprehend. 

What does love mean when you become a different person? Will it endure? Can it possibly endure?
