## The Willful Virgin
We live in a society constructed on patriarchy - it is male society we live in. Feminism is inherently against the patriarchy, but it is viewed that to go against the patriarchy in any way, to not desire men inherently in any way, is anti-heterosexual: is lesbianism. People both within and outside of gender studies claim that there is a correlation, or even a necessity for there to be, between lesbians and feminists. So: can you be a feminist if you are not a lesbian.
The author argues yes, imagining a certain virgin, both sexual and cultural, who only values their own independence and other women's support, who may even have sex without a man (just without any desire of a man). In this sense, she rejects everything patriarchal without being a lesbian.
## To Be and Be Seen
*The Politics of Reality*
Discusses how a phallocentric [[Epistemology]] has been constructed to leave lesbians in the realm of nonexistence (a topic of [[Ignorance]]). Provides a semantic analysis for how Reality in its linguistic and social sense has been constructed from the perspective of man - whose goal it is to wholly eradicate women (under the same argument as [[Janice Raymond]]). Women-identified-women is unintelligible under our current framework of knowledge (as is 'Karma' or a 'Round Square' in the English language). We need to pull back the curtains to reveal the performance of reality, and to see who has been subjugated to the background, to the stagehands. The lesbian cannot be defined, as the lesbian doesn't exist.
And yet she does...
