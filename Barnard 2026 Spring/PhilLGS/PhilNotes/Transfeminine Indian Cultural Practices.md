An extension of the broader discussion of [[Transgender History]]

Colonizers' accounts of South American indigenous cultural practices illustrates the prevalence of transfeminine identities within Indian societies. Such people would be brutalized and convicted of practicing sodomy. Third gendering (and inaccurately applying the term "Two-Spirit") of these people has been consistently employed over the centuries to cover up the existence of transfeminine expression and suppression. Transfems in these cultures mainly worked in bath houses or as sex workers.
