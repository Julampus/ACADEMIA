In Tom Léger and Riley MacLeod, [_Is There a Transgender Text in This Class?_](https://web.archive.org/web/20150116132626/http://topsidepress.com/media/Is-There-a-Transgender-Text-in-this-Class-1.1_pages.pdf)
	"Topside test"
	Is there a trans character?
	Do they know another trans character?
	Do the characters talk about something that isn't medical transition?
If it is yes to all three questions, it passes the "topside test"
[[Stone Butch Blues]] is treated as a monolith of trans literature
[[Redefining Realness]] is another type of trans memoir piece
[[Auto Anon]] writes Reverse Tomboy, a autofiction novel about trans butch identity
[[Susan Stryker]] collects history on trans people over the years
[[Torrey Peters]] writes dystopian short stories that explore the body horror of trans identity
[[Punks, Bulldaggers, and Welfare Queens]] by Cathy Cohen argues for an intersectional and individualized queer politics that argues for identity to be based on power
[[Whipping Girl]] by [[Julia Serano]] is a striking collection of essays discussing the trauma of transitioning, trans masculinity, transness in popular media, and the biology of transition
[[The Child Formerly Known as _____]] is a poem by Cameron awkward rich exploring trans mascness
