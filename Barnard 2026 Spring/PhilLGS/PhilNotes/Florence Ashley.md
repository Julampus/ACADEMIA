## Gender/Fucking
A erotic, theoretical series of essays and poems on transitioning and BDSM. Key ideas include the loss of transness upon getting SRS, the erotic as a creative blending of bodies (no longer defined genitalia but entire beautiful systems) together, "transamoury" and chasers as one in the same, abuse and consent, the ideas of topping and bottoming. Discusses [[Susan Stryker]]'s Monstrous bodies
