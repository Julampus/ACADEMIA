## Transsexualism
Transsexualism is little discussed in the sex conference, though a poem by [[Sharon Olds]] titled "Outside the Operating Room of the Sex-Change Doctor" makes its way into the program, the contents of which provide a distorted view of a male-to-female vaginoplasty, whereby penises are supposedly chopped off and lined on a tray. Olds conflates the penis as a symbol for masculinity and oppression, and so her conclusion is that the loss of a penis is a metaphorical laying down of arms, a middle finger to the father, a rejection of the flaming and untamed passions, and an apology to women.
