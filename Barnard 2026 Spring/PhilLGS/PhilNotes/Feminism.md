#philosophy 


In the canon, the 1st wave feminist movement primarily resonated on voting rights, concluding with the ratification of the 19th Amendment... naming the 2nd wave feminism "feminism" was a rhetorical choice that appealed to the victories of the 'first wave.' 
There was an interstitial addition to the cannon in 1949 by [[Simone De Beauvoir]], but it only "truly" started by 1968 with the Ms. America protests. This newer, broader wave was based on the foundations of the rigid 1st wave.

On gender, the move during the 2nd wave feminist movement was to go away from a gender essentialist definition of gender, but to recognize the class function of "man" as oppressor and "woman" at the bottom of the power hierarchy. Sexuality should be up for political critique - it is not as simple as gaining "equality" between men and women
