*survival, revolt, and queer antagonist struggle*

Context: STAR formed out of the occupation of Weinstein Hall (NYU) by a group of street queens in response to the GAA denying them entry to a queer dance. Groups of students also supported to protest. 
Note also that early queer revolution commonly excluded transgender people, and yet even Stonewall (started by femme queens) has been remarketed as a queer revolt.
[[Susan Stryker]]'s story of the Compton's Riot erasure aids to this understanding
STAR was violent at times and prioritized the homeless, drug users, and sex workers - a bitter pill for some to swallow. See [[Transgender History]] for a description of Stonewall (though note that it was in an area of the village with frequent police raids - except for when the mob was tipped off)
