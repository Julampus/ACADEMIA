[[Transcription]]al regulation
	In [[Prokaryotes]], requires promotor, transcriptional unit (DNA -> mRNA), and the regulatory sequences
	Operon - system with encoding for multiple proteins under control under a single regulatory signal/promoter - Polycistronic RNA given
	 Monocistronic (single gene unit) - 1 gene, 1 protein
	 In [[Eukaryotes]], post translational processing occurs
	 *Lac Operon* - positive induction
		 Lacz is a gene that encodes for an enzyme that breaks down disaccharides into monosaccharides
		 	 Lacz is promoted by CAP (catabolic control) + cAMP 
		 LacYA are also involved, though to lesser extents
		 Lac l is a gene that encodes for a regulatory protein which inhibits the transcription of lacz unless in the presence of lactose
			  Lac l is Monocistronic
	 Tpr operon - positive repression
		 W causes a positive feedback loop which produces more W
