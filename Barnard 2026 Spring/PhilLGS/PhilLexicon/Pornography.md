#philosophy 
The erotic in porn is power imbalance (in straight porn, at least)

In 1982, feminism blew up, in large part due to the [[Barnard Sex Conference of 1982]]. The main schism of the conference was around the subject of pornography, where the *radical feminists* argued that pornography was ultimately oppressive to the experiences of women, and where the ***women's liberation*** (or, derogatorily, "womens lib") movement saw the embracing of pornography as a necessity for the "sex-positive," sexual liberation agenda. 
Though the women's liberation movement's view of pornography won out in the end, it is worth revisiting the idea to be critical of how the "sex-positive" era of sexuality which we exist in now 1. establishes those who are uncomfortable about their sexualities as a defective "other," and 2. how the institution of heterosexuality might be intrinsically flawed due to the patriarchal system
