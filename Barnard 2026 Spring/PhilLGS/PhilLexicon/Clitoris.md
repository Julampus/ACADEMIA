Research into the clitoris, much like the female [[Prostate]], has been purposefully ignored for a great deal of scientific history. Starting out as a organ of potential significance for both sexual pleasure and secretion, the clit becomes vestigial throughout the years. Its size became underplayed (not merely a small nub, but rather a system of neuronic endings surrounding the vaginal canal) and its homology to the penis was lost. 
As it so often is, ignorance, and the context in which we come to understand the world around us, shapes the reality, function, and importance of material reality moreso than the tangible and empirical biological mechanisms at play. Re: [[Sexing the Body]] and Nancy Tauna's "Coming to Understand"
