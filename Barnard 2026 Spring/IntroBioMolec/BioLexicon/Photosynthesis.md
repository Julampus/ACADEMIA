**Light-dependent reactions** - Energy of sun converted to chemical energy in the form of NADPH and ATP; releases O2
	Electrons in chlorophylls and carotenoids absorb light energy - water is split releasing H+ and sending electrons over
	Photosystem II absorbs excited electrons derived from water
	Transferred to primary acceptor
	Electrons are transferred to photosystem I (electrons are re-excited)
	Energy is used to drive the cytochrome complex - H+ into the thylakoid lumen drives <u>chemiosmosis</u> through ATP synthase
	Electrons are then transferred to primary acceptor for NADPH
	Which is then sent to the [[Carbon Fixation (Calvin Cycle)]]
Cyclic ele 2ctron flow allows for the continuous formation of a gradient, sacrificing the production of NADPH

*Light independent reactions* - converts energy from previous reactions to fixate inorganic CO2 into organic C-containing materials
	3 CO2 + 6 NADPH + 5 H2O + 9 ATP -> G3P + 2 H+ + 6 NADP + 9 ADP + 8 Pi
	6 turns of the cycle makes 1 glucose
	Fixation -> Reduction (phosphorylation to create G3P using ATP, then is reduced by NADPH) -> Regeneration
