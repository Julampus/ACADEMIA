Intramolecular bonding

*Ionic Bond*
	A transfer of electrons form one atom to another, a charge difference attracts the particles together
	Ca**t**ion **+**
	Anion *-*
**Covalent Bond**
	A sharing of electrons, results in the formation of a [[Molecule]]
	Hybridization offers an inchoate solution to filling a valence shell
	==Nonpolar== - e.g. molecular oxygen, equal protons, equal electronegativity
	==Polar== - unequal electron sharing... e.g. H2O ([[Barnard 2026 Spring/IntroBioMolec/BioLexicon/Water]]; oxygen becomes more negative)

Large structures give rise to 3-Dimensional shape
