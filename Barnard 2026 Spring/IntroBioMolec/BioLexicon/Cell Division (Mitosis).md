#biology 

Goal is to go from one parent cell to two daughter cells

*Interphase* - for cell growth and preparation for mitosis
	G0 - non cycling cells that will not divide
	Gap1 (G1) - growth, microtubules develop
	Synthesis (S) - DNA replication, sister chromatids are made ([[Chromosome]]s), linked by cohesins ([[Protein (Amino Acids)]]), centrioles duplicated (two pairs of two centrioles make up two centrosomes)
	Gap2 (G2) - growth leading into mitosis
**Mitosis**
	Prophase - Nuclear envelope breakdown, chromosome condensation
	Prometaphase - spindle pole microtubules ([[Cytoskeleton]]) attach to the centromeres' kinetochore, centrosomes send these microtubules from either side of the cell
	Metaphase - alignment of chromosomes at midpoint, non-kinetochore microtubules push the two cells apart
	Anaphase - sister chromatids separate via. [[Motor Protein]]s, cohesins are removed
	Telophase - undoing prophase/prometaphase, nuclear membrane reforms, mitotic spindle is disassembled, kinetochore and non kinetochore microtubules removed
	Cytokinesis - two cells are formed with distinct membranes
		Actin filaments (Cytoskeleton) tighten around the furrow between the cells, pinches the cells until it is divided

Checkpoints regulate the cell cycle, passing the checkpoint promotes the next stage
Errors in checkpoints leads to [[Cancer]]
	G1/S checkpoint - check to see if anything is wrong with the cell
	G2/M checkpoint - seeing if DNA was copied correctly
	Mitotic Spindle checkpoint - making sure chromosomes and kinetochores are attached and aligned. 
Cyclins and CDKs (Cyclin-dependent-kinases) - cyclin buildup allow for the passage of checkpoints
	G1/S cyclin
	S cyclin
	M cyclin
