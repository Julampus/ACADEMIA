#biology

7-membrane spanner protein receptor, acts on associated G-protein

G-protein causes inactive GDP to detach, GTP to attach which, then detaches to phosphorylate a receptor (often adenylyl cyclase) that prompts the creation of cAMP from ATP, which triggers a cellular response after a phosphorylation cascade

Older than [[Receptor Tyrosine Kinase (RTK)]] - originating in prokaryotes
