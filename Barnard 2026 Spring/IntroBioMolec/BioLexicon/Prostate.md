The existence if a female prostate is hotly debated since the 1600's. There exist a set of heterogenous glands that resemble, histochemically and hormonally, that of the male prostate organ. Its putative relation to the orgasm and ejaculation of female subjects places it, in the nomenclature, close to that of the prostate. Previous terms to describe it included "Skene's Gland" and the "G-spot."
