#chemistry #biology 
*H2O*

Likes to form hydrogen bonds ([[Intermolecular Forces]]) with other molecules of water
	Liquid and Solid water form such bonds (a matter of heat in the system)

Temperature stabilizer - take a lot of heat energy to change the temperature of the water (so strong due to the hydrogen bonds) - "**Specific Heat**"

Forms surface tension - all water molecules at the top are pushed close together, as there is nowhere for them to go (without being ejected as a gas)

A polar solvent - dissolves polar molecules in it
What's more, forms distinct polar and nonpolar environments (See use case in lipid [[Barnard 2025 Winter/IntroBioO&P/BioLexicon/Macromolecules]])

Can dissociate into H+ and OH- => doesn't change the pH
