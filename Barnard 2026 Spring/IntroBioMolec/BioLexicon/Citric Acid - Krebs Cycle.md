#biology 

Continuing with the pyruvate from the [[Glycolysis]] process

Occurs in the mitochondrial ([[Mitochondrion]]) matrix - the cristae increases the surface area and thus, reaction amount, in the organelle

CoA (Coenzyme A; Vitamin B5 + Cysteine)
 
1) Pyruvate Oxidation (Occurring in the matrix)
2 pyruvate + 2CoA + 2NAD+ = 2acetylCoA + 2NADH + 2H+ + 2CO2
2) Citric Acid Cycle (Occurring in the matrix)
	2acetylCoA combines acetyl group to oxaloacetate from the previous cycle (CoA isn't used up)-> 2CO2 is released, 3NADH + 3H+, 1 ATP, FADH2
	Main reaction: oxaloacetate (4C) and the acetyl group (2C) from acetylCoA via. citrate synthase and water becomes citrate (6C)
	Citrate (citric acid) synthase negatively inhibits citrate synthase

4 NADH produced in total
