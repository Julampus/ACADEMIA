In Eukaryotes:
==Anaerobic==; Glycolysis -> Pyruvate -> // ==Aerobic==; Acetyl CoA -> Citric Acid Cycle -> Electron Transport System -> ATP Synthesis
## Overview
Re: [[Nutrient Cycling]]... Carbon cycle
	Carbon Cycle is made of two processes:
		Photosynthesis - synthesizing energy rich carbon-containing compounds ([[Polysaccharides (Monosaccharides)]], reducing CO2 and using light energy
		Cellular Respiration - breaking down food molecules via. oxidation in order to produce ATP

The multi-step nature of [[Respiration]] via. [[Reduction-Oxidation Reaction (RedOx)]] reactions allows for the utilization of small packets of energy for biological processes; an overall spontaneous process
	As opposed to combustion, whereby glucose requires a greater activation energy and only releases one packet of energy

[[Electron Shuttle]]s are utilized to facilitate the energy and electron requirements of the biological steps of respiration - NAD+/NADH; with the final point being an electronegative oxygen atom
## Glucose Respiration
Glucose + 6 O2 + 32 ADP + 32 Phosphate = 6 H2O + 6 CO2 + 32 ATP
1. [[Glycolysis]]
	In the cytosol; the breakdown of glucose to 2 pyruvate, 2 net ATP, 2 NADH are produced
2. Pyruvate Oxidation ([[Citric Acid - Krebs Cycle]])
	In the mitochondrial matrix; oxidizes pyruvate, converting it into CO2, CAC, ATP, and FADH2
3. [[Oxidative phosphorylation]]
	In the mitochondrial matrix and lumen; electron transport chain uses NADH electron attraction to oxygen to create chemical H+ and the synthesis of ATP

"Central Carbon Metabolism" - the intermediates of cellular respiration can be used to synthesize any macromolecule you can think of
Other macromolecules can join the respiration cycle at some time
