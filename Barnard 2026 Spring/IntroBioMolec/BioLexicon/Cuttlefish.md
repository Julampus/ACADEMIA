#biology  
In 2023 the Cephalapods received a name change, shifting up the of seppia to the genus level and making Ascarosepion a synonym of it

Coleoid cephalapods socially communicate via. camouflage (via. Chromatophores), as well as evade predation
Have green blood
Have a cuttle bone (large carbonate structure in the mantle) that aids in bouyancy
8 legs and two long tentacles for somatosensation
Sexual dimorphisms of fourth left tentacle in males (hectocotylus)
Closed circulatory system (like humans!)
Have three hearts, two for the set of gills and a systemic circuit
Release ink for evasion and mimicry
Color changing is learned and somewhat innate
Structural coloration (reflection from molecular/surface structure unlike pigment) 
	Iridophores refract light, causing iridescence (made from guanine)
	Leucophores reflect white light (made from guanine/adenine, more ordered)
Feed on shrimp
"Small males hide their sexually dimorphic fourth arms, change their skin pattern to the mottled appearance of females, and change the shape of their arms to mimic those of nonreceptive, egg-laying females" in order to mate with females, deceiving males that keep guard
Can be eaten, used as a dye to make sepia
Last common ancestor with vertebrates ~600 million years ago

Cephalopods have never been freshwater creatures

In particular, we work with ==*Sepiidae* - Ascarosepion bandense==
"The optic lobes of a dwarf cuttlefish comprise 75% of its brain by volume," seeing is everything
Have "W" shaped pupils
	The brain is split into 4 main areas:
		Subesophageal - below the esophagus
			For motor function and chromatophore control
		Supraesophageal - above the esophagus
			For complex thought
		Periesophageal - on the side of the esophagus
			Optic loves inhabit most of this region
		Nerve fibers - connecting the other three lobes
## Embryonic Development of the Camouflaging Dwarf Cephalopod, *Sepia Bandensis*; Montague et al. 2020

Can be used as a model organism due to quick development, efficient egg production, and small size
25 stages of development outlined

Skips a larval stage (different from other Lophotrochozoan sister taxa) and uses Hox for bilateral axial plan.

Have strong memory and exhibit synaptic plasticity as well as quantitative reasoning
Don't require parent care - die fast after mating

Can't manipulate the genome of the embryos in jelly -> have to find a way to remove it by adding bleach and carefully removing the jelly and ink

Stage 4 - asymetry arises
Blastocones on the periphery are larger than the blastomeres which make up the body of the blastodisc (center of the organism) 
	Located at the top lateral, animal (as opposed to vegetal) region of the embryo
Chorion is outer membrane of the embryo
Yolk takes up most of the embryo body in this phasae
Divide every 8 hours

After blastodisc is formed (stage 9) - germ layers form via. gastrulation
Starts enveloping the yolk
