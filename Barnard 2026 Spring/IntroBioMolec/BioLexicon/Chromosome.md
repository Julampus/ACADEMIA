#biology 

Chromosomes of the same type have similar genes in the same order, vary often by allelic type
Humans have 23 types of chromosomes (with two "sex determining" chromosomes, with many asterisks) - we have 2 copies of each set - contain genetic information... [[Nucleic Acids (Nucleotides)]]

Homologous chromosomes refer to chromosomes that encode for the same genes
Sister chromatids are copies of the same chromosome, often pairing to form the "X" shape characteristic of chromosomes.

Notably, X and Y sex chromosomes are not homologous, and so other machinery is required to allow for [[Meiosis]] separation
