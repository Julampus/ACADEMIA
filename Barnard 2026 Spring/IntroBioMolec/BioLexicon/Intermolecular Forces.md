#chemistry 

Electrostatic attractions or repulsions ==between== separate molecules

**Dipole-dipole** interaction
	Partially positive end of one molecule attracts to negative end of another molecule
	*Hydrogen bond* interaction
		Performs a type of dipole-dipole interaction in a much stronger way, as H resembles a proton (due to minor electronegativity)
		Able to perform this with Carbon, Oxygen, and Nitrogen
		Forms alpha helix ([[Protein (Amino Acids)]]) and the double helix ([[Nucleic Acids (Nucleotides)]]) - [[Barnard 2025 Winter/IntroBioO&P/BioLexicon/Macromolecules]] are stabilized by such forms
