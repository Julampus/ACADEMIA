Oxidative Phosphorylation (Occurring in the intermembrane space)
Electron transport chain uses shuttles to deliver electrons to electronegative oxygen, H_ ions are pumped out of the matrix and into the space
	Complex I - NADH drops electrons into the chain, pumps H+
	Complex II - FADH2 drops electrons at the second complex into the chain
	Complex III - Pumps H+ from electrons from I and II
	Complex IV - H+ pump, combining H+ in the matrix to make water with the electronegative oxygen (reducing concentration)
	ATP Synthase - works off of the H+ gradient
Chemiosmosis provides energy to ATP synthase (quaternary structure) to phosphorylate ADP to ATP
