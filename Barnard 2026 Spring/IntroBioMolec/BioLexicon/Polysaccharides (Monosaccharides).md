#biology 

Most abundant - most are sweet
Uses - energy storage, structural components, biochemical intermediates

Often composed in a CH2O ratio
	Mannose (6 Carbons; hexose), ribose (5 Carbon; pentose), glyceraldehyde (3 Carbon; a triose)

Aldehydes - double bonded O shares C with H
Ket*o*nes - oxygen to carbon alone
	Both double bonded

Monosaccharides exist linearly and ring shaped
	**alpha**-Glucose = inward, downward facing hydroxyl on C1 (step-wise bonding e.g. maltose)
	*beta*-Glucose = outward, upward facing hydroxyl on C1 (flipping bonds make a sloping molecule)
	Carbons numbered clockwise

Disaccharides are for transient energy storage (Similar to Random Access [[Memory]])
	Connections between carbon 1 and 4 (can exist 1-2, 103, 1-6)
	**Amylose** - **alpha** 1-4 bonded plant starch, glucose
	**==Glycogen==** - branched, **alpha** 1-4 and 1-6 linkages glucose molecules
	*Cellulose* - *beta* 1-4, structural material in plants (fiber), glucose
	*==Chitin==* - N-acetylglucosamine, linear *beta* 1-4 (cannot digest)
	
Starch = energy storage
