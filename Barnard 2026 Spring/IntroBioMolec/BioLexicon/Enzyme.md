Specific active site matches binding site of protein (tend to be 1 protein or 1 type of protein per enzyme)

Enzymatic activity can negatively impacted via:
	Competitive inhibition - binding to the active site and outcompeting the substrate
	Noncompetitive inhibition - binding to a secondary site to change the 3D structure of the enzyme
This same activity, when utilized by a cell, is referred to as, respectively:
	Nonallosteric
	Allosteric
