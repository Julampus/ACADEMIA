## Genetics
<u>Single-Nucleotide Mutations:</u>
*Missense Mutation* - a single nucleotide mutation that results in a single amino acid change
**Nonsense Mutation** - a premature termination sequence leads to translation of a truncated protein
==Silent Mutation== - a nucleotide mutation that results in no amino acid change
***Frameshift mutation*** - a nucleotide insertion or deletion leads to an entire ORF shift

<u>Large-Scale Mutations</u>
*Deletion* - Multiple nucleotides can be removed
**Duplication** - On DNA replication, a sequence is copied twice
==Reciprocal Translocation== - Chromosomes swap unequally (Crossing over of 1 <-> 3 nucleotides; Swapping ends of chromosomes e.g. the Philadelphia Chromosome)
***Inversion*** - Local nucleotides swap order
