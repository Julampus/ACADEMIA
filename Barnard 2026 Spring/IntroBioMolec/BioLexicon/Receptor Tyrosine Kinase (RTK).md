#biology 

Evolved newer than [[G-protein-coupled Receptor (GPCR)]] - in animals only

Dimeric cellular receptor containing 3 tyrosine each that, once phosphorylated; phosphorylation triggers the activation of adaptor proteins that cause the phosphorylation of  Ras via GTP; this triggers a kinase cascade that amplifies the signal (roughly 1,000x per kinase) and triggers transcription factors to enter the nucleus
See: [[Cellular Communication]]

e.g. insulin or mitogens (mitosis inducers)
