## Osmolarity/Tonicity
*Hypotonic Solution* - Lower concentration of solutes outside the membrane (e.g. distilled water)
***Isotonic Solution*** - Same concentration of solutes within and outside the membrane
**Hypertonic Solution** - Higher concentration of solutes outside the membrane (e.g. salt water)
