#biology 
*E. coli*
~4.6 mil bp
4288 annotated protein-coding genes
2584 operons
7 rRNA operons
86 tRNA genes

Clonally isolated (and thus work well in transformation and protein expression), cultured in defined media (water, carbon, nitrogen - auxotrophs can't grow on this media), can be infected by phages. 
Are able to conduct binary fission ([[Cell Division (Mitosis)]]) and horizontal gene transfer (rejoining and swapping DNA)

Help produce Vitamin K in the body (helps with [[Blood]] coagulation) - can also be pathogenic
