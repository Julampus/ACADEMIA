#biology 

Cancer is the uncontrolled proliferation of cells ([[Cell Division (Mitosis)]])
Multistep process - tumor mutations are selected ([[Natural Selection]]) via. random mutation

Proto-oncogenes are genes in normal cells encode for proteins that stimulate cell cycle
	E.g. ras activation - [[G-Protein]]
Tumor suppressor genes are genes in cells that inhibit division 
	E.g. loss of TP53 suppressor
Often occur from mutations in G1 cyclin pathway or due to telomerase suppression
