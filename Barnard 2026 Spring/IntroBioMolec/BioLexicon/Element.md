#chemistry 
A pure substance that cannot be broken down into simpler substances - E.g. the [[atom]]

The atom is composed of protons (+), neutrons (/), and electrons (-)
	Proton number is **atomic number**
	Proton + neutron = **atomic mass**
	Protons and neutrons bound by ***strong nuclear force***
	Electrons exist in outer *shells* of atoms, the orbitals - lots of space taken up, small size
		*Valence electrons* determine reactivity
