Water insoluble, non-polar (amphipathic - having polar and nonpolar regions) molecules made mostly of hydrocarbons

Energy, form membranes, biochemical intermediates
Made up of fatty acids (hydrocarbons, 14-22 in length) w/carboxyl group

Saturated - (with hydrogens) only single bonds 
	tend to be solid at room temperature, stronger van der wals forces
Unsaturated - (with hydrogens) having a presence of one or multiple double bonds 
	tend to be liquid at room temperature, weaker van der wals forces, prevents tight packing

CHO (and sometimes NP)

**Fatty Acids** hydrocarbons + *glycerol* sugar (via ester linkages, dehydration synthesis) = ***Triglyceride***
==Phospholipid== = two fatty acids, glycerol, and a phosphate group (makes the head highly polar) - in membrane synthesis
	Form bilayers and/or liposomes
Diversity exists within functional groups off of the glycerol molecule

"Dehydrated energy" - the saccharides bond with water, included in the mass

Steroids - polycyclic hydrocarbons (made of four carbon rings) 
	E.g. cholesterol 
	Mostly hydrophobic (slightly amphipathic)
	Signaling molecule - estradiol v. testosterone
