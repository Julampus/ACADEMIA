#biology 

The breakdown of glucose into smaller molecules - 10 reactions (catabolic, exergonic)

*1glucose* (6C) -> **2G3P** (Glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate; 3C) via. ==2ATP== (First 5 Steps; Energy Requiring) -> *2NAD*+ + 4e- + 4H+ -> **2NADH** + 2H+ -> 4ADP + 4phosphate -> 4 ==ATP== (substrate level from phosphofructokinase, phosphoglycerate kinase, pyruvate kinase, etc.; next 5 steps are energy releasing) -> ==2 pyruvate (3C)==

[[Enzyme]]s involved:
	Dehydrogenases - transfer electrons by transferring hydrogen
	Isomerases - enzymes that convert molecules to different isomers
	Kinases - phosphorylating proteins

"Substrate level phosphorylation"
