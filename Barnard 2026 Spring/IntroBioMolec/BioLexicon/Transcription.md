#biology 

The process by which DNA is read and transcribed into mRNA (or preRNA in [[Eukaryotes]]; to be processed into mRNA)
Gene expression (including [[Translation]]) occurs in the nucleoid for [[Prokaryotes]]

Transcription promoters generally have genomic motifs (e.g. TATA box)
In Eukaryotes: Transcription factors are factors that recognize 'promoters' and bind to DNA, then recruiting RNA polymerase (DNA-dependent RNA polymerase; creates phosphodiester bonds with DNA mediated by factors; writes 5'->3' relative to the sense DNA strand)
In Prokaryotes: The polymerase directly recognizes the promoter

Sense strand = DNA template
Antisense strand = Other strand

Initiation (dependent on promoter), Elongation (adding new base pairs), Termination (dependent on terminating sequence or terminating factors bound to the DNA)

Notably, RNA can base pair with itself to create hairpin structure (termination sequence)

Prokaryote mRNA can be transcribed to create multiple proteins (for translation)
Eukaryotic mRNA is processed, with a G protein cap and a poly-A tail (for mRNA half-life), and splicing out introns

5' UTR and 3' UTR are untranslated regions
