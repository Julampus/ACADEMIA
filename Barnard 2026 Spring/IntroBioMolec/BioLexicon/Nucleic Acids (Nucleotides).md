## Biochemistry
Polymers of nucleotides - 3 phosphate groups, a sugar, and a nitrogenous base (reminiscent of sterol, though has 2 rings rather than four)
Individual parts (Sans phosphate) are called nucleosides
Forms antiparallel ladder where the direction of the strands form a dimer in opposite directions

<u>Structural organization</u>
DNA (2nm) -> Nucleosomes (10nm; DNA strand wrapped around the histone proteins) -> Forms solenoids (30nm) that then coil to make chromosomes

Two main types of nucleic acids:
	DNA (deoxynucleic acid) - hereditary material of all living things (and some viruses)
	RNA (ribonucleic acid) - intermediary of protein synthesis (made from [[Translation]] and involved in [[Transcription]])
Vary by which 5 Carbon sugar is used

**Ribose** and *deoxyribose* vary by a presence of **OH** or *H*, respectively, on the C'2

Made up of sugar-phosphate backbone... dehydration synthesis (removing water and two phosphates), makes a ***phosphodiester*** bond
	3'C to 5'C (phosphate) bond 

<u>Pyrimidines (Pyramids cut)</u> - have one ring
Cytosine (cytidine) - 3 H bonds with G
Thymine (thymidine) - 2 H bonds with A
Uracil (uridine) - in RNA, with A (2 bonds)

<u>Purines (Pure as gold)</u> - have two rings
Adenine (adenosine) - 2 H bonds with T (U in RNA)
Guanine (guanosine) - 3 H bonds with C*
## RNA-Specific
preRNA (in eukaryotes) - directly transcribed from DNA
mRNA - post-transcription modified RNA (or final product in prokaryotic preRNA)
snRNA - form snRNPs (small ribonucleoprotein) used in splicing for RNA processing
RNA polymerase I - synthesizes rRNA
RNA polymerase II - synthesizes ribosomal "mRNAs"
RNA polymerase III   - synthesizes tRNAs, rRNAs, snRNAs
	(In prokaryotes there is only one RNA polymerase)
tRNA - transfer RNA that moves the amino acids (binds via. the anticodon) -> conforms to secondary structure (binds to itself)
rRNA - Huge ribonucleoprotein complex made of ribosomal ([[Ribosome]]) RNA and proteins
	3 Sites: A - aminoacyl/attach site, P - peptidyl/protein site, E - exit site
