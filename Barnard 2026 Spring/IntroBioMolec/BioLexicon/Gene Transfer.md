In [[Prokaryotes]]

*Transformation* - DNA absorbed through DNA from dead bacteria
**Generalized transduction** - genes that are transferred are random via. phages
==Specialized transduction== - genes that are transferred can be predicted because bacteriophage integrates into donor genome
<u>Conjugation</u> - Active transfer of plasmids from one live cell to another via. F pilus (The f pilus is encoded by the f plasmid - itself encoded by the plasmid)
	Maybe it isn't the bacteria's goal to replicate genes, but the plasmid itself a. la. [[Virus]]es
	Recombination across plasmids occur
	A whole genome takes ~90 minutes to transfer
	Two different strains:
		F+ - Presence of pilus and conjugal bridge (does not transfer chromosomal DNA; isolated F+ plasmid)
		Hfr - High-frequency of recombination (F+ gene is integrated into the chromosome)
