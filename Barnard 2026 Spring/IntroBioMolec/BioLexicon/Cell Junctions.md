#biology 

In [[Animalia (Kingdom)]]

Anchoring Junctions (via. the [[Cytoskeleton]]):
	Desmosomes - connected to ***intermediate filaments***
	Adherens - connected to *microfilaments* 
	***Intermediate filaments*** skewer plaque in membranes of cells
Cell junctions - (not connected to the [[Cytoskeleton]]):
	Tight junctions - tight seal between cells so nothing can pass between (e.g. [[Epithelium]] and [[Stomach]])
		Fuses membranes together
	Gap junctions - channel connections between cells to allow passage of ions (similar to plant plasmodesmata; e.g. [[Neuron]]s)
		Cylindrical arrays of proteins
