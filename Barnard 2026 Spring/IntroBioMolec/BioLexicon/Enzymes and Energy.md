## Thermodynamics of Life
Energy - the ability to do work
ATP is the primary molecule involved in cellular work

Delta G = Delta H - T(Delta S)
Where:
	G = Gibbs free energy
	H = Enthalpy (stored potential energy in a system)
	T = Temperature (in Kelvin)
	S = Entropy (disorder)

**2 Types of Reactions (Relating to Free Energy)**
	Exergonic - overall negative gibs free energy change (-H, +S)
	Endergonic - overall positive gives free energy change (+H, -S)

*2 Types of Reactions (Relating to Metabolism)*
	Catabolic - energy is released (e.g. hydrolysis, combustion, etc.)
	Anabolic - energy is stored (e.g. dehydration synthesis)
