### Organic Chemistry
***Organic Molecule*** - Molecules that include carbon (excluding some molecules like CO2)
	Macromolecules are ***Organic***
	[[Carbon]] has 4 valence electrons (A# - 6) 
	*Hydrocarbons* - just carbon and hydrogen
		E.g. Methane (CH4) ... Ethane (C2H6), Ethylene (C2H4), Acetylene (C2H2), Benzene (C6H6)
		Can be linear or branched - diversity of hydrocarbon structure
		Mostly *nonpolar*
		Unreactive... though combustible
		==No oxygen whatsoever!==
	*Hydrocarbon* + **Function Group** = New properties
		E.g. Hydroxyl (OH), Carbonyl (CO), Carboxyl (COOH), Amino (NH2, Phosphate (PO4), Sulfhydryl (SH), Methyl (CH3)
	
Chemical Reactions:
	Hydrolysis - destruction via water
	Dehydration synthesis (Condensation) - combination, creating water
Isomers - same chemical formula but different structure
	Constitutional isomers - same chemical ferule, different order
	Stereoisomer - Same formula and connectivity, but are arranged differently around central carbon (enantiomers), four different things (even if it is carbons with different electronegativities)
