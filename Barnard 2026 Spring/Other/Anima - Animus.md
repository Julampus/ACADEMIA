#psychoanalysis 

A [[Complex (Imago)]] that exists, in the female body, as *animus*, and in the male body, as *anima*. 
The anima/us has a relationship to the ego, whereby it is the gendered converse that one displays in the [[Persona]]. E.g. a masculine, macho guy will have a developed inner anima and vice versa with women. 
The anima/us is not only a [[Shadow]]ed version of persona expression, but also a projection of the archetype of "woman" or "man," "masculine" or "feminine." It is an idealistic [[Symbol]] based in the [[Collective Unconscious]] that often falls short of being matched in love. 
Anima/Animus can be seen as a psychic extension (based in unconscious [[Archetype]]) of the somatic sex-drive. It is what calls us together; it is union not only with body but in spirit: Man with woman, anima with animus, the wise man and the chthonic woman. In this way anima and animus form a dialectic, as does anima and ego. They conflict to synergize into progressive union/regressive destruction and psychic wholeness (the combination of the [[Conscious]] and [[Unconscious]])/uncontrolled chaos depending on how temperate/compatible the opposing sides are.

It is important to note that this theory predicates on the fact that the image of the feminine is inherent to a woman's ego, and the image of masculinity is inherent to man. This is a view often dubbed oppositional sexism, whereby passivity, nurturing, and emotion is contrasted with penetration, strength, and reason. Though there is some biological basis in social necessity (i.e. parents should be nurturing, emotion is valued for connection, reason is good for survival), dividing these virtues into separate groups is unreasonable.
