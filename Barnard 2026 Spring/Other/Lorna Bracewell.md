#philosophy 

Discusses the capture of the pro-sex movement (a. la. [[Gayle Rubin]]) under the project of  '[[Liberalism]],' broadly construed as the idea of freedom of individual expression (freedom to lie) and freedom of markets. Pornography, in this sense, was captured and placed under the purview of [[Capitalism]], now an industry of the state to be controlled, regulated, and profited off of.
