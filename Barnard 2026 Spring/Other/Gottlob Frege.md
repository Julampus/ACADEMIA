#philosophy 

Philosopher of [[Analytic Philosophy]], established the foundations of formal logic which would be used by [[Bertrand Russel]] and the other analytic philosophers. Became a nazi

Rejected [[Idealism]] and psychologism - that things like induction, causation, etc. are just products of human psychology - as with [[G.E. Moore]]
	Failed in a lot of ways to disprove certain propositions and largely ignored [[Skepticism]] to his own detriment. Still, he was influential to [[Ludwig Wittgenstein]]'s philosophy
