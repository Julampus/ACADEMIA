#psychoanalysis 

To [[Sigmund Freud]], *libido* is a drive to fulfill sexual desire (that which all desires arebased on)

For [[Carl Gustav Jung]], libido should not be understood to be limited by sexual desire. Rather, Jung argues that libido is *psychic energy*: the energy which is transferred and flowed from unconscious, conscious, and soma.

While the [[Complex (Imago)]], their constellation, and the [[Ego]]-[[Conscious]]ness' collision with the internal and external world is described causally, the energic world of libido is a more physical, probabilistic description of psychic happenings. Energy flows from areas of high concentration to areas of low concentration, following the general pattern of what we know as entropy (Movement from low to high complexity, order to chaos.) In this sense, psyche can be viewed as an open system that moves towards an increase in entropy (as external collisions are possible; bodies do not exist in isolation) - and yet near the end of one's age the opposite is observed, in which a sort of psychic stasis is observed and psychic order is achieved (a healthy mind).

Libido often functions in terms of **progression** and *regression*, whereby psychic energy my be in constant motion towards goals (sex drive, production, creativity, etc.) or away from others (depression, sloth, hedonism, etc.). These means of progression and regression of libido manifest in different ways depending on the [[Psychological Type]] an individual embodies

Though the libido often has its origins in the biological imperative (as we all start with natural drives towards sex), we tend to commit ourselves to things that are completely devoid of this notion (i.e. work culture in Japan and lowering birth rates). In this sense we sublimate our libido, still transferring our psychic energy into the ego-consciousness and the systems around us, yet the object changes. 
What causes this change of focus? We as humans, Jung argues, do not have separate cultural and natural states. Rather, the two are inseparable. We are born with great skills to analogize via. language and [[Symbol]]

The libido originates in the psychoid realm (from [[Instinct]]/[[Archetype]], where the image arises and is interpreted through these understandings) - before being translated by the [[Collective Unconscious]] and transferred into the [[Unconscious]]. Here, complexes are formed. Once constellated, they transfer libido into the [[Conscious]] where soma and ego receives the data, equating to some equivalent output. The unconscious contents can also come out of the unconscious through stimuli/dreams, or come into the unconscious via. repression.

For [[Jaques Lacan]], the libido in its purest state can be imagined as the 'lamella' or an independent organ of the body clinging to its humanity; that autonomous, immortal thing which continues going. Even more so, the libido acts out through its [[Death drive]]
