#psychoanalysis 

The psychic content that we are aware of, that we know is the ego

Development of the ego occurs through collisions with reality
 
The ego can decide which psychic contents stay within consciousness and which are "repressed" into the unconscious
The ego shapes the center of consciousness, it is inherited at birth - the eye that sees becomes an individual, the neurons that fire form fields in fields

An ego (a particularly weak one) can be co-opted by emotion, desire, and identification

The ego is a powerful tool of the psyche -> the operator

The ego is continuous? Potentially; the "I" that was 3 appears to be the "I" that I am today -> Is the "I" eternal? There appears to be an intuition that it is (or a desire for this to be) the case

The ego depends on the somatic image (which depends on the body) and the psychic (one would presume that the ego could exist without the body, just as it can without memory, though we have a profound fear of death; a fear of losing our "I")

Inhabits the field of consciousness though draws from the psychic content of the unconscious
	Can even disassociate from consciousness, to some degree

Collision of the ego causes growth, resilience, strength
	Trauma can damage and regress the ego; a collision too hard will cause deep, psychic damage
Internal and somatic collisions affect the ego differently

Can be hijacked by the [[Complex (Imago)]], illuding to the fact that our personalities are merely the summation of personality fragments in our conscious/unconscious. We only notice the change in control over our own ego - a seeming loss of free will (conscious -> unconscious action/desire/fantasy/emotion)
