#psychoanalysis 

Are not a result of ego-consciousness, but rather something more inherent

2 Main Ones:
	Introvertedness - the understanding of what is going on within, having a proclivity to solitude and mental development
	Extrovertedness - the understanding of what is going on in the world around you, a proclivity to socialization is often associated with this
		Note it is not that a person is born as simply one or the other, but rather that they are two forces with varying weight over an individual's psyche. One is not completely devoid of socialization just as one is not completely devoid of self enrichment. It is merely the primary way in which an individual comes to approach the world. Conflict arises when the opposing method is valued for too long, resulting in psychic damage as the cost of forcing the secondary factor up
4 Secondary Ones:
	Intuition, feeling, thinking, and sensation
