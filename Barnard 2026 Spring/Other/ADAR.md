*Adenosine deaminase acting on RNA*
Ribonucleotide A->I enzyme

ADAR1 - p150, Deaminase activity, Zalpha (lacked by p110 variant) & Zbeta binding sites, nuclear export signal (lacked by p110 variant), Z binding sites may play role in innate immunity ([[Immune System]]) regulation. Also contain dsRBD (double stranded RNA binding domains) 1, 2, and 3
ADAR2 - Deaminase activity, dsRBD 1 and 2... more highly researched
ADAR3 - Does not exhibit deaminase activity, dsRBD 1 and 2

ADAR is extremely positively charged (likely to facilitate binding to RNA)
ADAR in squid are edit their own transcripts
