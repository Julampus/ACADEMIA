#philosophy 

A psycho-biological imperative made up of:
	*Goals* - what one desires to ***attain***
	**Effort** - work required to achieve ***attainment***
	***Attainment*** - as in attaining one's *goals* 
	==Autonomy*== - for many, it is a prerequisite in order to obtain power and fulfillment from attaining a goal. Not just effort, but autonomous effort.
Often mimicked a [[Surrogate Activity]] once biological needs are met (i.e. in modern society)
