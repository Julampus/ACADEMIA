#philosophy 
A purely ethical philosophy, denoting how one ought to live one's life

A way of life, a [[Philosophy]], coming from the Greek word "Cynic," or "dog." This life is characterized by  an appeal to simplicity, asceticism, and a clear and peaceful mind (ataraxia). This is often embodies through the renouncement of wealth and the pursuit of philosophy, both cures to the physical problems while attempts at resolving the mental ones.

Notable cynics include [[Diogenes the Cynic]], and *Antisthenes*, and Zeno of Citium (who later became the founder of [[Stoicism]]), all of whom followed after the [[Plato]]nic tradition
