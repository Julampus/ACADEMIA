## Jung's Map of the Soul
By *Murray Stein*

The [[Ego]] (the "I") exists at the center of the field of [[Barnard 2026 Spring/PhilLGS/PhilLexicon/Consciousness]] (enveloped in somethig)

The [[Psyche]] is the totality of the [[Conscious]], [[Unconscious]], and [[Mind (Nous)]]

The Conscious is a field, to Jung. Within the field of consciousness resides the [[Ego]] - the empirical personality; thought, desire, will, and action (what we see to be the self)
The ego is subject to the psyche. That is, the psyche "reflects," or even "projects" certain experiences onto the surface of the ego, making it apparent to the personal "I"
