#philosophy 

The philosophy of language is the study of how meaning is conferred, and what exactly (linguistic) meaning is. Inspired by [[Ordinary Language Philosophy]], [[Ludwig Wittgenstein]], formal logic, and the early [[Logic]] of [[Aristotle]]

Notable names include [[Donald Davidson]], Dummet, and [[Saul Kripke]]
