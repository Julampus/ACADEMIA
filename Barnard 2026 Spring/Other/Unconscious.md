#psychoanalysis 

The psychic content that lies outside, as Jung would say, the "Field of Consciousness" - the psychic content we are unaware of (though presumably identical to the [[Conscious]] psychic content, except for its conspicuity)

Those contents that we can voluntarily call forward (unto the ego, in the consciousness; e.g. memory)
The contents that we cannot voluntarily call forward  (unto the ego, in the consciousness)
The contents that are unable to be called forward at all

Within the unconscious lie "satellites" and "comets" of [[Complex (Imago)]]es
