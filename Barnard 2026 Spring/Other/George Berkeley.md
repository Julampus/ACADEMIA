#philosophy 

An Anglo-Irish philosopher, idealist ([[Idealism]]), empiricist ([[Empiricism]]), and Christian

"For a thing to exist is for it to be perceived" - That is, a mind is imprinted by an idea, and we can only know that the idea exists, so all perceptions exist. Early proponent of idealism to combat the rising epistemological skepticism (in order for Berkeley to back empiricsm) and theological skepticism (in order for Berkeley to defend the existence of God). God exists, Berkeley argues, as an infinite mind that observes us and the external world to preserve continuity.
