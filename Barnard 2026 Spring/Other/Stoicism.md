#philosophy 
Followed in the tradition of [[Cynicism]], though differed in the sense that it was more practical (didn't call for the rejection of society; likely also more profitable) and as such was less individual-focused (not merely prudential, but emphasized honour)

The post-Socratic philosophy founded by Zeno of Citium (and later followed up by Seneca, Marcus Aurelius, and Epictetus), that argues for the pursuit of an *apathetic* life. That is, a life that is completely in the hands of the individual, whereby the individual is not acted upon by the passions (greed, lust, desire, etc.) or even the "indifferents" (those things that can be good or bad depending on context; wealth, health, fortune, etc.) and acts to pursue [[Virtue (Arete; De 德)]] (temperament, courage, wisdom, justice).
