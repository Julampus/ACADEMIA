#philosophy 

Linguist and philosopher of [[Continental Philosophy]], also studied [[Aesthetics]] and [[Hermeneutics]]

Discusses the phenomenological concept of art as play, where just as a player of a game becomes immersed in the rules of the game and seemingly gains a new experience, or a driver of a car seemingly expands one's experience to envelop the car, so too does art incorporate us into it as a player of its world.
