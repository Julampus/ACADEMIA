#philosophy 

An artificial *goal* one develops to not fall into psychological despair (created often when one's biological needs are met and attained)
One doesn't feel deprived if one loses out on a surrogate activity (unlike food or sex)
As opposed to a necessary activity (one that fulfills a part of the power process naturally; one necessary for survival), surrogate activities, once completed, simply move the goal post farther back (are often more unsatisfactory)... I suppose the same could be said for a biological imperative
	Though, there are ends in a day's meal earned, whereas a research paper often leads into the next (and is over a much larger span of time)
