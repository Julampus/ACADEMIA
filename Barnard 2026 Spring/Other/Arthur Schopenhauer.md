#philosophy 
*to live is to suffer*
19th Century philosopher, proponent of [[Pederasty]], hater of women, and detractor from [[G.W.F. Hegel]]

Schopenhauer rejected Hegel's evocation of the World [[Spirit (Geist)]], claiming it merely to be the same hand-waving that religions would utilize (of which there is some truth to this). Rather, Schopenhauer embraced the Kantian ([[Immanuel Kant]]) model of reality as mediated by an empirical and rational notions. Schopenhauer claimed that the makeup of the [[Noumenon]] is accessible as [[Will]] (departing from Kant) - the origin of appetite, desire, and [[Conscious]]ness.

Schopenhauer was deeply inspired by [[hinduism]] and [[buddhism]]. Specifically, he was inspired by the notions of life as suffering ([[Samsara]]; Jerma would say "Life is Pain!") and the desire for [[moksha]], or release from the cycle. He was deeply concerned with [[Ethics]], and as such proposed an Epicurean ([[Epicureanism]]) emphasis on pleasure and compassion. Once we recognize the shared suffering across all organisms (even animals) and share compassion, we can lessen the suffering and thus do good/lead a good life. 

Schopenhauer argued that art, and especially music, are ways to alleviate suffering by capturing the good in the aesthetic experience and by promoting compassion.
