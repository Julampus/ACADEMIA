#philosophy 

The belief that empirical data (that is, data derived from observing the world through the senses, often aided by technology) provides us a way at accessing truth.
A problem of [[Skepticism]] arises which [[rationalists]] like Descartes utilize, where we are able to doubt whether what we are perceiving is an accurate representation of what really exists. In fact, it is true that we often do not get the full picture (yet it is the goal of science to elucidate what our limited human bodies can detect). 

Main figures of empiricism during the [[Enlightenment]] include [[John Locke]], [[George Berkeley]], and [[David Hume]]
