#philosophy 

Most popular in the anglophone world, it follows the empiricist ([[Empiricism]]) and rationalist ([[Rationalism]]) traditions laid out by Kant. The schism in thought by the turn of the 20th century resulted in analytic and [[Continental Philosophy]] breaking off.
Emphasized the study of [[Logic]] (and the emergence of formal logic), science, philosophy of [[Language]]/[[Mind (Nous)]]/science, the natural sciences, math. This happened at the same time as the "linguistic turn" and the emphasis on "meaning making" via. language (as [[Semiotics]] also began to develop)

Even more simply, continental philosophy was expressed through the literary tradition, while analytic philosophy was expressed through the technical/academic tradition
