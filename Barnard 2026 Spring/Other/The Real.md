#psychoanalysis 

For [[Jaques Lacan]], 'The Real' does not imply a Kantian noumenon, an external world existing outside our reach, but rather the many varying environments we construct in our mind, the many worlds and their laws which we follow

'the small thing a' is that little splinter that produces individuation - the [[Ego]] and the other even when similarity overwhelms difference; this is the site in which we gain the effective meaning of difference
