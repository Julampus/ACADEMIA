#psychoanalysis 

In the Freudian ([[Sigmund Freud]]) sense, the superego is those negative desires which, contrasted by the ego-ideal in its goodness, seemingly sabotage the self. The [[Death drive]] manifests here, that is the absolution of humanity towards immortality or unfeeling.

In the Lacanian ([[Jaques Lacan]]) sense, the superego is less so the negative desires which make up the mask which conceals our [[id]], but rather the illusion that there could be desires beneath the mask of the 'I,' the [[Ego]]. The superego is neither good nor evil - this binary Lacan deems far too simplistic- but rather the affirmative/imperative 'Desire!'
