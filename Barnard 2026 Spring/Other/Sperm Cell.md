#biology 

Head region contains acrosome (modified Golgi), a cap-like structure containing lipolytic enzymes that allows delivery of the pronucleus
The gametic DNA is wrapped not around histones of regular nuclear DNA, but rather around protamines (homologous function)
The mitochondria are located on the elongated distal region of the sperm head - their mtDNA has been degraded so their only function is metabolic
