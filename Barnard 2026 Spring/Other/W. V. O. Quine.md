#philosophy 

Also referred to as the "van" - wrote ~20 books
American logician ([[Analytic Philosophy]]) inspired by [[Bertrand Russel]] - mostly covered semantical work. A member of the *Vienna circle* - he embraced positivism

Discussed 'gavagi' and the separation between sense and reference.

Extensional - meanings (synonyms) are able to be swapped (e.g. Cicero and Tully) *salva veritate*
Intensional - this is unable to occur
Quine argued for the former

Naturalist - believed that natural sciences provided the best explanation of reality (though that our epistemology is fallible)
Physicalist - believed that everything that can be said can only be described in physical terms
	These notions influenced [[Saul Kripke]] later on
