#philosophy 

A catch all term for all philosophy that did not follow the analytical tradition ([[Analytic Philosophy]]). Developed around the 20th century, and includes [[Phenomenology]], [[Existentialism]]. If analytic philosophy was following the Kantian tradition, then continental philosophy moreso followed [[G.W.F. Hegel]], though this is an oversimplification, of course.

Even more simply, continental philosophy was expressed through the literary tradition, while analytic philosophy was expressed through the technical/academic tradition
