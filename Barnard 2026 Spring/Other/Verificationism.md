#philosophy 

In the philosophy of science, verificationism is the belief that knowledge is confirmed by observations that reflect such knowledge and are infirmed by an absence of such observations

A big part of logical [[Positivism]] - [[Karl Popper]] later points out the logical fallacy in this:
	p->q; q ... p
	You can't assume the antecedent from the consequent
	Valid but not true - [[Logic]]
Popper argued, thus, for rejectionism - modus tollens over ponens
	One could say this is just [[induction]] in disguise
