#psychoanalysis 

The part of the ego that is expressed publicly (though not always in accordance with the [[Ego]]-[[Conscious]]ness). The persona embodies the traits which the [[Shadow]] lacks, often being temperance, morals, intelligence, etc.

The persona develops over time, as does the individual, often taking on a role that society expects of the individual. This "mask" can become grafted onto the ego-conscious experience, though may be ultimately contradictory to the individuals innate desires

[[Friedrich Nietzsche]] would likely say that the persona has been influenced by the slave morality, and it is actually the shadow (and thus the wills) that we should pursue in life.
