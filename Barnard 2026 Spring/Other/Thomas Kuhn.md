#philosophy 

Philosopher of science - Scientists work under paradigms... when the paradigm fails to corroborate with observation, a new paradigm is invented and a **[[paradigm shift]]** occurs (Mysticism -> Alchemy -> Newtonian -> Relative). Corroborates with [[G.E. Moore]]'s holism

Commiserates [[Karl Popper]]'s views - knowledge across paradigm shifts are incommensurable ('electron' pre and post quantum mechanics)
