#philosophy 

Pyrrho of Elis, the founder of this school, was written to have had traveled to India in Alexander the Great's army, where he returned with a metaphysical philosophy not unlike that of the Hindu tradition, whereby:
The world doesn't really exist as it presents itself/is an illusion (thus, our beliefs which stem from perception and experience cannot be known to be true of false). Thus we should have no views about anything

This aligned with the school of [[Skepticism]] which was emerging at a similar time

He resembled [[Diogenes the Cynic]] in the sense that he didn't give a GAF (walked by a guy drowning, would walk into oncoming traffic)
