#philosophy 

The love of wisdom, as the greeks would call it
Philosophy is also the way to analyze the world through a critical lense for the sake of progress ([[Philosophy As an Antidote to Injustice]]) - "Philosophy is the antidote to the uncritical acceptance of the world and ourselves as we are"
It is the search for truth, for knowledge, for finding out what knowledge is ([[Epistemology]])
It is the quest to find what a good life is, what it looks like ([[Ethics]])
It is finding out the true nature of the world ([[Metaphysics]])

Also included as further areas of study are [[Philosophy of Language]], [[Philosophy of Science]], [[Feminist Philosophy]], [[philosophy of mind]], [[Political Philosophy]], [[Aesthetics]], [[Logic]] etc.

The [[Philosopher]] is someone who studies philosophy
