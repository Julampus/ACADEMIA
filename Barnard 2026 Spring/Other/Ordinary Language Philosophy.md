#philosophy 

The connecting tissue between [[Analytic Philosophy]] and the philosophy of [[Language]] - inspired by the works of [[Ludwig Wittgenstein]] and the members of the vienna circle. Placed an emphasis on the linguistic/semantical origins of philosophical though

The theoretical basis of [[Speech Act]]s

Describes
	Locutionary acts - doing anything with languages
	Illocutionary acts - act of stating, acting, or commanding
	Perlocutionary acts - the effect achieved by saying something

Notable names include Ryle, Austin, and Strawson
