#psychoanalysis 
A boundary region of the unconscious, whereby imperative transitions gradually into controlled will
	Its upper bound is the spiritual [[Archetype]]
	Its lower bound is the biological [[Instinct]]
Between the two bounds of the psychoid is the psyche, firm and given form.

The *psychoid*, a term borrowed from Hans Driesch, defines a semi-lucid region which psychic energy ([[Libido]]) inhabits
