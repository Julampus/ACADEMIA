### Some Principles of History
Several principles must be understood on History:
1. A small impact on a long-term trend will result in transitory change (the trend, as trends do, will balance out to its original state and move in accordance with societal evolution); society, too, must be changed
2. A large impact, able to change a long-term trend, will change society
3. Such changes cannot be predicted, long-term (unless there exist analogues for such change)
4. You cannot plan out a society and then make changes according to such a plan
5. Societies are formed irrationally, through societal action

Revolution cannot be predicted, cannot be planned, if it is big enough to actually change something
