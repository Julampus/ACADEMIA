#philosophy 

A philosopher of science in the tradition of [[Analytic Philosophy]] - Part of the vienna circle

Was greatly influential in the field of logic, mathematics, and science in the first half of the 20th century. 
Separated logical (a priori) scientific theories with those that are illogical (empirical and theoretical both the two resting on conditional postulates). The issue is that we have reason to believe that all scientific theories are illogical under this framework.
