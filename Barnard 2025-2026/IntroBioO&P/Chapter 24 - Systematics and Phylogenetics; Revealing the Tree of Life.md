A way of visualizing the explicit and implicit relationships between species over time. 
Branching points in *phylogenetic trees* are called **nodes**
**Nodes** with monophyletic lineages (complete coverage of all branching points from a single point) are called [[Clade]]s

Monophyletic taxa discovered by structuring the phylogenetic tree to reveal monophyletic taxa
