#biology 

Organized organelle system
Double-helix DNA

Tends to be considered as *monophyletic*

**Endosymbiotic Theory**:
	Heterotrophic cell eats a proto-[[Mitochondrion]], forms a symbiotic relationship
	Secondary endosymbiosis occurs when heterotrophic cell with mitochondrion eats [[Cyanobacteria]], fails to digest the cell but lives in [[Symbiosis]], forming the first of the [[Eukaryotes]]
