*Miller-Urey* experiment
	In 1953 - tried to copy what early earth was to see how life could've formed with a closed system
	Boiling water and water vapor would interact with an electrode (mimicking high lighting-activity in early earth) and atmospheric gases => amino acids condensed from this mixture
	Demonstrated the origins of life, [[Macromolecules]] formed
	Notably, the *water cycle* ([[Nutrient Cycling]]) prompted this to occur

**Protocells**
	Self organizing pre-cellular structure from fatty acids... able to grow and absorb materials osmotically
	Likely formed spontaneously from silica surface interactions
	Can reproduce themselves with RNA, are naturally variable thanks to mutation, have fitness differences

***Tracking origins of biology in the field***:
	Stromatolites - the oldest living thing, microbial mats of [[Cyanobacteria]] ([[Plankton]]) dating back over **3.5 billion years** 
	Isotope ratios - chemical signatures in ancient rocks that suggest life i.e. with bone formation
	Microfossils - looks at the fossils of cells
