**The Free Will Problem**
1. We have free will
2. Determinism is true
3. Free will doesn't seem compatible with determinism

[[Determinism]] *is not* fate, but more of a linear path, where each starting point has a unique end point

Contemporary philosophers think about free will in the sense of what you are in "control" over
	Control helps you find where the free will is
	I.e. with the *principle of alternate possibilities* - [[Harry G. Frankfurt]]
	Is free will relevant to having alternate possibilities, is it just a matter of being coerced or not. Inevitable BUT free actions. If its not about alternate possibilities, then was is it about?!
	A matter of looking backwards at previous decisions and how they influenced your decision
		Rather than looking forwards at how it diverges (like [[David Lewis]])
