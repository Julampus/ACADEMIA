"Time travel, I maintain, is possible."

An issue with how a supposed time traveler can travel for, lets say, 1 hour, and appear months or years in the past. It appears as though potentially two different dimensions of time are present, one for the regular human and one for the time travelerю

It is easier to argue that time is one dimensional, one of the 4 dimensions in which we occupy (the other three being the spatial dimensions)
*Change* is qualitative difference between temporal stages 

*External time* - that which occupies a certain role in the pattern of events that comprise the course of the universe

*Personal time* - that which occupies a certain role in the pattern of events that comprise the time travelers life
	Properties continue to change consistently
Time travelers travel across the line of time in a ne dimensional zig-zag, overlapping themselves over and over
