[Playboy Interview](https://www.synthforbreakfast.nl/wendy-carlos-playboy-interview-1979/) - *1979*
Wendy Carlos, inventor of the Moog Synthesizer (an electronic instrument that is able to produce sounds, of which can be modified from a default sine wave, to produce and mimic tones) and composer for the "Clockwork Orange" and "The Shining" as well as the album "Switched on Bach" (an electronic cover of various pieces by Johan Sebastian), is interviewed by playboy magazine. She was formerly known as Walter Carlos and appeared under this name as a public, semi-famous figure. 
Wendy reflects on her coming out experience and divulges information about her SRS (a process of interviews very par-for-the-course of the 90's in which it was written - more so a fascination with pathologization and the body than of the experience and the individuality of the subject) There is a certain perversion of the interviewing (mirroring that of the mases) in that a majority of the interview centers around the gruesome details of surgery, sex, etcetera.
There is a certain pressure that public figures in particular face when it comes to transitioning of balancing expectations, curiosities, and assumptions of their audiences (like the average trans experience on steroids) 
She is a Columbia grad, btw!
