 ## Lab Description
The purpose of this lab was to analyze the relationship between flexor and extensor muscles (**antagonistic pair**; e.g. bicep and triceps, respectively) in different exercise motions: the push-up and the bicep curl, as well as investigating the effect of wearing a mask on breathing activity (performed through jumping jacks) measured in breathing rate over 15 second intervals
Overall, what was found was that there was no significant difference in breathing rate when wearing a mask and when not wearing a face mask in both resting and exercise states. We also saw a significant relationship between bicep flexion and triceps extension during a bicep curl, and vice versa for a relaxing motion, using an *electromyogram* (measuring the net neuronal activity within the specific muscle groups; voltage). The pushing motion of a push up extends the triceps and flexes the bicep.
