## Lab Description
Making both quantitative and qualitative measurements to see if soil substrate has any effect on [[Asteraceae]] germination rate, growth height, and shoot length. Notably, the analyzed sunflower lengths and germination rates did not vary across all groups (Soil, + gravel, +woodchips). From the resultant p value from the **ANOVA*** test, there was no significant difference between any of the experimental groups. This confirms the null hypothesis that substrate variance has no effect on the germination or growth rates in sunflowers.
## Notes
Though a *t-test* is typically used to compare two different groups and their means (as was used in the [[O&P Lab 2 - Insect Ecology]]), an **Analysis of Variance** (**ANOVA**) must be performed in order to compare the three types of soil plots. 
	Still assumes the null hypothesis, like a t test
	Built upon *between-group variance* - the variance of one specific variable across all groups - and *within-group variance* - the differences between the means of the groups that is not due to the specific variable

$$
Fvalue = x / y
$$
where x is the *between-group variance* and y is the *within-group variance*
Larger F values = smaller P values

Post-hoc Pairwise Comparisons need to be conducted if resultant P value is smaller than 0.05 - tells you where specifically the differences are
