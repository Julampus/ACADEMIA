#biology 

*Pituitary* - Regulates other endocrine glands, located in the [[Brain]]
	Reproductive timing (tied to [[Behavior (Biology)]]) though controlled bodily through hormones
	Controls Gamete Production
	Anterior gland:
	Luteinizing hormone (LH) - released by the pituitary, regulates reproductive systems
		Stimulates the production of testosterone in the gonads, acts on Leydig cells
		Way to remember: largening hardon
	Follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) - works in tandem with LH
		Stimulates spermatogenesis
	Posterior gland:
	Produces ADH - see [[Kidneys]], and many other hormones

*Gonads* - Produce gametes and sex [[Hormone]]s

*Adrenal* - Releases stress hormones that aid in predation evasion or short-term survival

*Pancreas* - part of the digestive system ([[Chapter 47 - Digestive Systems and Animal Nutrition]])
