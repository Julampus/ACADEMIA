Two types of variability:
Constant - consistent across all classes of antibody
Variable - at the tip, the differing amino acid allows for the specificity of the binding site to the epitope

### Translation of Antibody
During B cell differentiation, the same constant region of the gene coding for an antibody merges with a varying variable domain and unique joining region - forming a singular gene for a new, unique antibody
### Function of Antibody
Bind to pathogens or antigens, neutralizing (disabling their function) or agglutination (immobilizing pathogen; creating a web of the pathogen-antibody construct)
