#biology 

Divided into **rough** and *smooth* ER (this state is malleable)
**Rough ER** involved in [[Protein (Amino Acids)]] synthesis
	Ribosomes are imbedded in the membrane of the Rough ER
	Ribosomal imbedding is transient
	Proteins are imbedded into the ==lumen== of the ER via. [[Translation]]
*Smooth ER* is involved in [[Lipid (Fatty Acids)]] synthesis
