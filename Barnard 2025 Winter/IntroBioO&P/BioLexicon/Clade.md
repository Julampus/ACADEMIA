#biology 
As pertaining to phylogenetic trees.

Creation of clades is dubbed cladogenesis

***Root*** - common ancestor of species within a clad
A way of visualizing the explicit and implicit relationships between species over time. 
**Node** - Branching points in *phylogenetic trees* 
[[Clade]] - Nodes with monophyletic lineages (complete coverage of all branching points from a single point)
	==Sister [[Clade]]s/Taxa== - Share eacho others closest relatives
Branch - the interval of time between speciation and ==tip== or other **node**
==Tips/Terminals== - currently living species or taxa
