#biology 

40% Grey Matter - located on the outside (cortex) of the organ; doing the "thinking" speech. learning, etc.

==60% White Matter== - located on the inside (beneath the cerebral cortex) of the organ; transmits info between the grey matter and the rest of the body, works in conjunction with the spinal chord

Nuclei - clusters of thousands of [[Neuron]]s in a neural circuit

Brain & Reflexes:
	Simple input-output stimulus-response reflexes

Part of the *CNS*

Highly selective (runs purely off of glucose and ketones) thanks to the blood-brain barrier

Suspended in protective cerebrospinal fluid and meninges [[Tissue]]

***Hypothalamus*** - main hormone regulator, signals to the pituitary gland and other peripheral glands in the body through neuronal activity
	Releases ADH in the [[Kidneys]] - Antidiuretic hormone
