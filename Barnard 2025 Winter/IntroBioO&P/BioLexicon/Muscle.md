Type of [[Tissue]]
![[TypesOfMuscle.png]]
*Skeletal muscle* - attached to bones of the skeleton and connective tissue (**tendons**), S-shaped cells - voluntary
**Cardiac Muscle** - (constantly in use, involuntary; in the heart) 
***Smooth Muscle*** - came first in the Cambrian explosion, locomotion allows for evading predation
Work on contraction (movement of overlapping myofibrils), extension, and rotation, slower atp contraction... varying numbers of nuclei across all muscle types

Muscle fibers run the length of the muscles, have multiple nuclei
### Function
Flexion - (lifting a weight), contracting muscles (overlapping bands; called the sliding filament model, depends on interaction between actin and myosin)
Extension - lowering, relaxing muscles (increase in tropomyosin)
### Structure
![[Muscle.png]]Muscle -> Muscle bundle -> Fascicle -> Muscle Fiber (the muscle [[Cell]])-> Myofibril (Fiber connectors)

==Myofibrils== - smallest components within the muscle (the "atom" of a muscle)
	**Thick** filaments - parallel bundles; act as an oar (attached to thin filaments). ATP binds to the **myosin** of the thick filaments, myosin binds to thin filament's actin.
		Myosin pulls on the actin, thick acting on thin
	*Thin* filaments - If tropomyosin is present, the muscle isn't able to contract (for resting muscle). Once it's no longer present, attachment between the filaments can occur. *Actin* binds to **myosin**. ***Tropomyosin*** blocks these binding sites on actin

Striation Bands
	Part of of myofibrils, measured in a sarcomere
	Proteins work together to make movement
	A bands - dark bands where **overlap** occurs (containing the M line)
	H zone - **thicker** filaments within the zone, lighter middle region of the A band
	M line - **hold** together the stack of thick filaments (M for Maintain)
	I band - light. **thin** filaments (containing the Z lines, I for )
	Z line - where the thin filaments are **anchored**, the z bands (Z for Zig Zag, thin )
The bands overlap one another... the lines hold each other together
ZIAHM (Line, band, band, zone, line)
A connects to Z (like amazon)

Sliding Filament Model
Ca2+ released from [[Sacroplasmic Reticulum]] -> Initiates contraction by stimulating Troponin to remove Tropomyosin
**Neuromuscular Junction** (NMJ) - synapse between the motor [[Neuron]]s, triggering action potential

More signals -> more contraction
