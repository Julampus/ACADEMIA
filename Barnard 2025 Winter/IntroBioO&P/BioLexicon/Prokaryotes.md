#biology 

*E.g. Bacteria and Archaea*
*0.5 - 1.0 microns*
## Core Features
Circular DNA localized in nucleoid region
[[Ribosome]] in cytoplasm
Can have cell wall
Constrained by size: SA:V ratio (ought to be spherical)
	As shape gets larger, SA:V ratio drops
No membrane bound [[Organelle]]s
Tend to have 1-5x10^6 bp long circular chromosome

[[Bacteria]] in particular - gram+ / gram- classification
	Hans Christian Gram - used CV to stain bacteria (gram+ would be stained purple - binds to ***peptidoglycan*** - polysaccharide + amino acids)
	**Gram+** = cell wall present on the outside of plasma membrane
	*Gram-* = cytoplasm -> plasma membrane -> thin wall -> outer membrane -> capsule
		==Glycocalyx== in some *gram-* bacteria (e.g. capsule... [[Polysaccharides (Monosaccharides)]])
			Capsule - regular glycocalyx
			Slime layer - less regular layer
	==Motor protein complex== - whip-like structure allow bacteria to move through liquids
		E.g. flagella/cilia
	==Pili== - hair like shaft, often used in motor function, communication ([[Quorum Sensing]]) and genetic transfer

Diversity found in:
	Shape: e.g. Sphere, rod, corkscrew
	Environmental niches
	Metabolic requirements
