#biology 

***Hematocrit***
Erythrocytes - Red blood cells, transport oxygen, contain [[Hemoglobin]]
Leukocytes - White blood cells, defend from pathogens
Thrombocytes - Platelets for healing

Bright red blood, saturated with oxygen
Deoxygenated [[Tissue]]s lack oxygen

*Veins* - bring deoxygenated blood back to the [[Heart (and the Circulatory System)]], small and thinner cell wall due to less pressure
**Arteries** - bring oxygenated blood away from the *heart*, bigger than veins
***Capillaries*** - an intermediary to transfer from artery to vein

==Plasma== is everything else in the blood (proteins, ions, pH regulators)

A and B antigens have higher susceptibility to malaria parasite (form rosettes that promote parasite spreading)
O has higher susceptibility to cholera
