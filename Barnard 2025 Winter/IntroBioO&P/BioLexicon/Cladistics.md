#biology 

Studying [[Clade]]s, specifically the morphological, molecular, and behavioral characteristics across species

Studying its character state (present or absent)
	Represented visually with a cladistic table, with 1's indicating a present trait and 0's indicating an absent trait

*Derived* character states - a newly evolved trait
*Ancestral* character states - trait present in ancestor

**Synapomorphy** - the shared trait that defines a [[Clade]]
