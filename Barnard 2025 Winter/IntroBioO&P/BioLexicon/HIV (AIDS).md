Autoimmune deficiency caused by HIV (Human Immunodeficiency [[Virus]])
Initially assumed to only be present in gay man (labelled "*GRID*" or Gay-related Immunodeficiency), as rich gay white men were the first ones to be seen suffering this disease. It is now known to be transferred through any transfer of bodily fluids (so those having heterosexual sex aren't spared; in fact, it is a homophobic myth that it only occurs to gay people), and is most often seen transmitted among intravenous drug users

Though people don't die directly from the virus, even being able to live with it for several years to decades (though most often 3 years, untreated)... the AIDS symptom causes the body's immune system (See [[Immune System]]) to shut down, leading to the vulnerability of many other diseases in its stead (e.g. rheumatoid arthritic, lymph swelling, etc.)
