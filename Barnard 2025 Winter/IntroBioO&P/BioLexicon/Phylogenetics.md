#biology 

The study of the evolutionary relationships between species based off of observable traits

*Monophyletic*
	All organisms/species descended from a given node, and only those
	How phylogenetic trees tend to be structured
**Paraphyletic**
	All organisms/species descended from a given node with a single monophyletic group missing
	What is excluded is called the "outgroup" -> what does not have the cladal character state ([[Clade]])
***Polyphyletic***
	Includes some but not all organisms/species that share a common ancestor
