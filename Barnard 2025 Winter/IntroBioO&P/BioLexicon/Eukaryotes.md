#biology 
Eu - true
Karyote - nucleus
e.g. "True Nucleus"

*10-20 microns*
*e.g. animal, protozoan, plant, fungal, etc.*
## Core Features
Organized organelle system
Increased surface area through folds and membrane bound [[Organelle]]s
	e.g. SA:V ratio cheated, making them able to be larger than [[Prokaryotes]]
Double-helix DNA
Nuclear envelope (double membrane to protect and compartmentalize [[Nucleus]]) - lumen between bilayers is consistent with [[Endoplasmic Reticulum]]
Nuclear lumens - make up cytoskeletal network - facilitate transport using nuclear pore complex
Tends to be considered as *monophyletic*
Have ~3.3x10&9 bp of linear chromosomes
Have necessary transcription factors which prokaryotes do not have for [[Transcription]]
### Plant Structures
Contain cell wall, granum, chloroplasts, and a [[Vacuole]] in addition to other eukaryotic key features
In the [[Cell Wall]] - [[Polysaccharides (Monosaccharides)]] called lamella between different cells dotted with plasmodesmata for water transport
## Transport
Pore complex for nucleus allows for selective transport across membrane
Nucleolus - where ribosomal RNA is transcribed and processed (much like the nucleoid region in prokaryotes)
**Endosymbiotic Theory**:
	Heterotrophic cell eats a proto-[[Mitochondrion]], forms a symbiotic relationship
	Secondary endosymbiosis occurs when heterotrophic cell with mitochondrion eats [[Cyanobacteria]], fails to digest the cell but lives in [[Symbiosis]], forming the first of the [[Eukaryotes]]
## Endomembrane System
In a sense, the membrane bound organelles function as "continuous" with the [[Plasma Membrane]]
