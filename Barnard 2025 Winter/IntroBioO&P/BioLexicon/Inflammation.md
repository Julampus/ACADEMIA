#biology 

Common Local Responses:
Increase extracellular fluid (*edema*)
Increase temperature
Tissue healing
Increased arrival of white blood cells
Dilated blood vessels and formation of pores (interstitial spaces) for monocytes to flow through

Entire Body Response:
Lymph nodes pass fluids (lymph nodes filled with macrophages + lymphocytes)

Autoimmune diseases never shut off - constantly target healthy [[Tissue]] in the body
