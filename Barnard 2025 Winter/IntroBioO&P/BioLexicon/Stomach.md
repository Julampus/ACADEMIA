#biology 
An [[Organ]]
#### Structure
Three layers of smooth [[Muscle]] (longitudinal, circular, and oblique)
Churns and mixes food into chyme
#### Function
Uses chemical and mechanical processes

A lot of folds exist on the surface of the stomach, allow for maximum surface area and thus, higher nutrient absorption

Pepsin (prompted by Chief cells) begins enzymatic breakdown of proteins into smaller peptides

Digestion process of Bio-[[Barnard 2025 Winter/IntroBioO&P/BioLexicon/Macromolecules]]:
Carbs begin to break down in the mouth (involving salivary response)
Proteins begin to break down in the stomach (involving pepsin)
Fats are primarily broken down in the small intestine (lipase)
