#### 40.1 - Organizing Neurons into Systems
See [[Neuron]]s![[CNS and PNS.png]]***Neural Circuit*** is a collection of neurons sending and receiving signals (similar to a computer circuit)
	Feedforward excitation: a neuron sends a signal to another neuron in the chain
	Feedback excitation: a neuron triggers a previous neuron in the chain that leads to the original neuron being triggered
	Feedback inhibition: a neuron triggers its own inhibition
Their activation can be seen through **fMRI** (function Magnetic Resonance Testing), which detects increases in oxygenated hemoglobin in activated neurons (which cause a slight change in magnetic force relative to deoxygenated hemoglobin)

#### 40.2 - The Peripheral Nervous System (PNS)
*Peripheral Nervous System* (*PNS*)- glands, ganglia, etc.
	Relays info between the CNS and the rest of the body
	Sensory input + motor function = Peripheral Nervous System
	Connection of the 31 spinal nerves to the 12 cranial nerves

***Somatic Nervous System*** - Controls motor neuron function and [[Muscle]] locomotion, receives sense data

***Autonomic Nervous System*** - Projects to glands to release hormones and enzymes
	*Parasympathetic Division* - Ganglia near the brain, stem, and spinal tip
		Activated in low stress situations and to relax the body
		Often the suppressor to signals triggered by the...
	**Sympathetic Division** - Spinal Column
		Activated in high stress situations or excitement

#### 40.3 - The Central Nervous System (CNS)
**Central Nervous System** (**CNS**) - ==brain== and spinal chord
	Integration, processing, and coordination of sensory data and motor commands
	***Blood Brain Barrier*** - regulates what enters the brain organ (blocks fatty acids cannot cross it efficiently), brain relies on carbs, mostly [[Brain]] [[Tissue]]
	CNS projects efferent neuron signals to afferent neuronal ganglia in the PNS
