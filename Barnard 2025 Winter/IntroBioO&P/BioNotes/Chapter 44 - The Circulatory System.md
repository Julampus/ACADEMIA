### 44.4 Blood Vessels of the Circulatory System 
The insulin-glucagon loop is highly conserved across vertebrates, as varying levels of glucose are required through the day, and maintaining a stable level of glucose helps one perform basic functions.
### 44.5 Maintaining Blood Flow and Pressure 
*Pancreas* - releases insulin... (A [[Gland]]; See [[Chapter 42 - The Endocrine System]])

**alpha cells** - raise blood glucose levels
	Insulin is decreased and glucagon is increased if you have low blood glucose levels
*beta cells* - lower blood glucose levels
	Hormone released: insulin
	Once you eat a food item, insulin will be released by these beta cells in order to lower the blood glucose spike

See [[Diabetes]] to see how glucose intake affects the circulatory system in bodies with ineffective alpha/beta cells or an ineffective pancreas 

[[Natural Selection]] favors organisms that maintain stable glucose level (because this is proportional to stable brain function)

Why we have the Circulatory system:
	To allow growth of the organism
	To carry oxygen throughout the body and remove the CO2 waste
### 44.6 The Lymphatic System
Lost fluid from the intravenous system is brought to the lymphatic system, where the interstitial fluid is recombined with the main circulatory system
