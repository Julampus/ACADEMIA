Animals that feed on highly digestible foods have shorter digestive tracks
	E.g. meat takes shorter to digest than plant matter (carnivore vs herbivore tracks)
	No wasted space!

Functions of excretory system:
	*Homoregulation* - pH, nutrient, ion concentration, removal of metabolic waste
	Selective absorption - reabsorbing what we can so bodies are as efficient as possible
	Removal of nitrogen based waste (through urea)
	Autoimmune diseases often target kidney function
	[[Kidneys]]

The main goals of the excretory system are to retain
	Homeostasis in pH, ion concentration, nutrient levels
	Remove toxins and metabolic wastes (ammonium, urea, etc.)
