How is it that there still exists a creationist movement even after creationist theories keep getting debunked (since the theory of evolution?)

As opposed to the theory of science, religious creationists and fundamentalists argued for a class called "*Intelligent Design*" that would teach creationist ideas to school children
"Intelligent Design," then, is the theory that we can see evidence of some intelligent creator (always argued to be God) based off of the intricacies and complexities of the biological systems around us
	Originated from William Paley in the 1700s
	Nowadays, its not only creationists who are intelligent design advocates (in fact, they are mostly in conflict with one another)

This idea is, though not as talked about today, mirrored with the argument of the supposed "constants of the universe" (gravitational force, strong/weak nuclear force, electromagnetic force) being tuned just right so that life can emerge, a clear piece of evidence for intelligent design

There exist numerous examples of **un***intellegent* *design* (rabbits eating their own feces, the human heart only built with one artery (coronary))

Teaching creationism violates the separation of church and state - first decided in the *Scopes-monkey trial* - intelligent design is posed more so as a theory rather than any sort of religiously affiliated belief

There are certainly holes in Darwinian evolution (such as how the entire flagellum, in all its intricacies, could've evolved)
