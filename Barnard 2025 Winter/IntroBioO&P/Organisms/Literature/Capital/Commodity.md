#economics 

A form in which an object takes one under a capitalistic system that is characterized by having both [[Exchange-Value]] (price; money) and [[Use-Value]] (utility; utils.) - a thing that satisfies a human desire/need
	Can be thought of as a product which is traded and exchanged in a commerce-based society.
	Or even as a [[Data Structure]] containing both aforementioned properties.
	i.e. if it can't be exchanged for something else, it isn't a commodity
The value of the commodity is *fetishized*; made to appear as real as the object itself

An example of a product of labour that is not a commodity can be a shovel made in a communal, societally-focused society. The shovel does in fact have a [[Use-Value]], but its ownership and [[Exchange-Value]] is undefined and thus cannot be a [[Commodity]]

See [[Karl Marx]] for general description of the alienation of the product; the commodity
