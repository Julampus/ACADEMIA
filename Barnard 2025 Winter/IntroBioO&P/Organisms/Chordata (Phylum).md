#biology 

**Characteristics of Chordata**:
1. a notochord - flexible structural support (replaced by spinal column in vertebrates)
2. a dorsal hollow nerve cord - often develops into the brain and spinal chord in vertebrates, located dorsally (on top of) the notochord
3. pharyngeal slits - openings posterior to the mouth, often used in filtering food (develop into the jaw and ears of humans)
4. post-anal tail - used to help with locomotion (swimming) or balance (vestigial in apes and humans)
All of which can be seen during embryonic development
