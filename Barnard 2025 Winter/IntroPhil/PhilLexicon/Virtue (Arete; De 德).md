#philosophy 
From the [[Analects]]
De, 德 - Virtue or Power

The power received and mandated by the Heavens for following [[Rites (Li, 禮)]]
Functions as a compass, guiding you to the [[Way (Dao, 道)]]

From [[Sappho]], must be in balance with wealth to achieve happiness

In the traditional Greek tradition: courage, temperance, wisdom, justice
