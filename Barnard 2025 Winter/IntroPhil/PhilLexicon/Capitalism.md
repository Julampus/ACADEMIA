#economics 

[[Karl Marx]] - A way of organizing society by which production is executed in the pursuit of [[Capital]]
	Whereby the means of production is owned by the *bourgeoisie* (the capitalist) and lent to the **proletariat**
	People who do not own the means of production sell their labor for ==wages; money== - the capitalist owns the object
	Free trade, a free market, and (ideally) no government intervention are involved
	A system of private properties and private ownership
	No welfare/Social services (these are privatized)
	Profit incentive
	Top down [[Wage]] relationship (e.g. from CEO to Manager to Worker)
Viewed in contrast to [[Communism]]

Distinct from the Lockian notion of property, as the value imbued within the product doesn't necessarily make it the individuals
Marx's origin (contra the [[State of Nature]]) is the *original accumulation* - a violent act of seizing the means of production
