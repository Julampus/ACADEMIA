#philosophy 

## [[Thomas Hobbes]]
To Hobbes, the state of nature is a timeless period of "every man against every man", where life is "nasty, brutish, solitary, and short". People are governed, not by society (which does not exist), but by the natural laws, where no man can go against one's own desires, and natural rights, where every man can lay claim to what one's PASSIONS desire. It is truly a state of LIBERTY, where all are free to do as they please. BUT, there is no security.
