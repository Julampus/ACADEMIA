#philosophy 

White lies could be *pragmatic*, rather than an *epistemic* ([[Epistemology]]) reason

Embraces **[[Fallibilism]]** - the epistemic belief that all beliefs have the potential to change if subject to new information
What *is* is only what something can *do* - e.g. hardness in a stone is only the ability for it to scratch, withstand pressure, etc. (similar to [[Teleology]])
In terms of beliefs, [[Truth]] is only a label we use for beliefs that work/are useful in their given context
Solves an issue of [[Skepticism]] in that skepticism itself, as a belief, can be true and valid insofar as it is useful
