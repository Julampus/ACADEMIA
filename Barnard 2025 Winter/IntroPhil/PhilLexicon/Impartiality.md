"...one would do for others as one would do for oneself." - universal love

A Mozist thought - a version of the [[Gentleman (Junzi, 君子)]], though discussed in [[Mozi]]
	As opposed to a partial person (a Confucian) who constructs hierarchy based off of goodness and chooses (or is pushed by the [[Way (Dao, 道)]]) not to interact with commoners
Maybe the argument formed is a form of [[Game Theory]]... best benefit for all, tit for tat
	Is this goodness for the world, or [[Good (and Ren, 仁)]] for the sake of [[Virtue (Arete; De 德)]]
