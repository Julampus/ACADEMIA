#philosophy 

In the [[Plato]]nic sense, a dialectic is the confrontation of two claims which both act on each other to get closer to [[Truth]] (e.g. Thrasymachus and Socrates in [[Republic]])

To [[G.W.F. Hegel]], the dialectic is not only present in dialogues and knowledge-gaining, but is the mechanism by which all change occurs, all new ideas are made, and by which consciousness becomes self-aware.
*Thesis* is violently confronted by **antithesis**, with ***synthesis*** as its result. Syntheses collide with syntheses in a continual process
