#philosophy 

Greek Ideas of Love:
Romantic [[Love]] - [[Eros]]
Love from trust - storge 
Love of higher beings, of the universe - [[Agape]]
Playful love of young couples - ludus
Maddening love - mania
Loving and accepting yourself - philautia
A lifetime's love, enduring - pragma
Love from your closest friends - [[Philia]]

In [[The Symposium]]
Beautiful bodies -> All bodies -> Mind and soul -> Knowledge and Virtue (law) -> the form of beauty

In [[Notes from the Second Year]]
A political love is formed, distinct from any sort of erotic/romantic love; a love of sisterhood, solidarity, and shared struggle
