#philosophy 
By [[Friedrich Nietzsche]]
*Sec. 125. The madman*
"God is dead. god remains dead. And we have killed him. "How shall we comfort ourselves, the murderers of all murderers?"
Where "killing" god is simply the nonbelief in God; the main question is not whether God is dead, but what we are to do with ourselves now that we are untethered from such a comfort

We now exist in a world where our morals and ethics remain detached from absolute truth, and thus we must find some other basis for our values (and potentially in order to find purpose, though he came before the wave of philosophers studying [[Existentialism]])

God's relevance has waned, it is you who makes the choice to believe in God or to shape the meaning of man - man takes responsibility
