#psychoanalysis 

Discusses the [[Big Other]] and a linguistic approach to psychoanalysis. While [[Sigmund Freud]] discussed psychoanalysis as an empirical tool for discerning the mechanisms of the psyche, Lacan was looking at mapping the transcendental nature of the psyche as limited by [[Language]]. It is not only what is symbolically signified in an action, a word, or a [[Sign]], but the presence and performance of the action.

Often times, in action, we can become disimpassioned by our actions, feeling moreso the surrogate emotion than the real. We ought to stop asking ourselves "Who am I?" as this always comes at the expense and whim of the big other.

In terms of *desires*, we seemingly have none (at least on the external, social level), or at least, these desires have no meaning or value unless ascribed value by the big Other. The illusion is just that: that we ask ourselves "What do I want" when what we mean is "What is it that others (the Other) wants of me"
