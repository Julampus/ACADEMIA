#philosophy 

Proto-[[Existentialism]], Danish Christian [[Philosopher]]

Wrote [[Fear and Trembling]], among other works

Proposed the idea of the *Anguish of Abraham*
That is, how do you know if it is an angel (or God, or some authority higher than you) that speaks to you, and not some malicious or simply false message? How do you know if you are who it speaks to you? You must take a [[Leap of Faith]]
