#philosophy 

Wrote [[Thus Spake Zarathustra]]; [[The Gay Science]]; and [[Twilight of the Idols]]

[[Master-Slave Morality]] - Inspired, in part, by [[Arthur Schopenhauer]]'s philosophy of the fundamental nature of the [[Will]], and human's role in taking control and overcoming - becoming the [[Ubermench]]
In saying "God is Dead," Nietzsche was emphasizing the dwindling of Christianity's authority in morals, virtue, and metaphysics, saying and affirming that it is the place of the individual to discover those things for themself.
Life is suffering, in the Schopenhauerian sense, and we should face such with courage rather than humility. 

Said that all art embodies either the [[Dionysian-Apollonian]] dynamic - a [[Dialectic]] between irrationality and reason, hedonism and order
	Art teaches us how to live beautiful lives - we become poets of our own lives - to overcome, to live
