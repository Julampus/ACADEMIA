		Knowledge is already within, the potential (e.g. the slave boy discovering mathematical proofs)

	Not so much about finding the answer, but finding holes in ones knowledge

Believed in the preexisting essence of things -> prioritized finding the right way to live over the nature of reality (ethics vs. metaphysics)

It is the mind that has any authority over the body, it is the mind that makes us who we are, not the body.

Did not develop a formal school like Plato or [[Aristotle]] - informal and radical teachings

A well lived life is one where intellectual vigor is taken in its highest regard
