## ESSAYS
### Poetry is not a Luxury
Poetry is not merely wordplay, not merely a lesser form of thought to rhetoric, to logic and enlightenment texts, but the birther of thought "as dreams birth concepts and feeling births idea and knowledge, understanding"
It is the distillation of the indescribable, of feeling and the subjective moment.

But language and ideas are not new, just unearthed from being forgotten, or rearranged to be anew -> dreams will only remain dreams unless thought turns to action
	Otherwise are children's dreams will be the same as ours

Feelings were expected to kneel to thought as women were expected to kneel to men.
	A key idea that I believe Ding studies in her feminist philosophy

For there are no new ideas. There are only new ways of making them felt  (a counterargument that can even be applied to [[artificial intelligence]])

Poetry cannot be treated as a luxury, it must be armed by the people to reappropriate feeling, the universal shared experience, for the masses
	In a way, ideas are the oppressors

Lorde references the "white fathers" - a metonymy for the enlightenment thinkers and American founding fathers
Uses imaginitive, poetic language in her philosophy, blurring the line between the two
### Uses of the Erotic: The Erotic as Power
See [[Eros]]; very Jeremy Fragrance

The erotic is the sensation *with* the feeling, sensation alone is hedonism
Part of the feminist suppression is the vilification of the **erotic**, to Lorde - the naturally female. Patriarchy depends on the emphasis on the idea (something that only the preexisting ruling male class can determine to be intelligent or produce to mass appeal), rejecting the innately feminine as either the pornographic and unsanitary or overly emotive and irrational.

"The aim of each thing which we do is to make our lives and the lives of our children richer and more possible"
	Though thought provoking and having biological/behavioral origin, this ultimatum does not account for the asexual, aromantic, or separately, the anti-natalist experiences

Erotic power is robbed in a world where value does not come from human value but from the value of [[Capital]] and profit - the erotic, in its current state, is more often than not precarious, dangerous, and horrifying
"The severe abstinence of the ascetic becomes the ruling obsession. And it is one not of self-discipline but of self-abnegation."

The erotic is power, though its power has been undermined for the ascetic, mental life that patriarchy imposes as a tool to generate capital without proper production. The subjugated class is disabled by coming to fear or disdain or reject their own power in the romantic, sensational, and transcendent
