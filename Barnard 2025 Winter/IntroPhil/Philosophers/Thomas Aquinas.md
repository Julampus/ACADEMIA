#philosophy 
Of the theory of the **First Causer**
![[St-thomas-aquinasFXD.jpg]]
Argued that, though we cannot fully comprehend God's infinity through his finite effects, the very existence of his effects prove his own existence. In *Summa theologiae*, a massive work spanning millions of words.

Made an argument in [[Ontology]]
	The first mover argument, causality and the first mover/cause
	An "unmoved" first mover must be God

Described a clear outline between form and substance, meaning that an individuals true substance is what is to be judged and not the shape that takes place (existence and essence; as a leaf changes colors but still remains the same leaf). Additionally, Aquinas' concept of divine grace given to the individual prompts problems of [[Free Will]], as if God chooses who will be good and who won't, then how can we say we are able to make choices about our existence, sin or virtue.
