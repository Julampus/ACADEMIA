#philosophy 
*1515-1582*
The first individual (as opposed to [[René Descartes]]) to use a demonic figure to demonstrate an initial *a priori* [[Truth]]

A Spanish Nun

Though she was incorporated into the Jesuit canon, she was forced (due to her religious pressures) to wrote in a more round-about way in order to not be censored

This is the depiction of Teresa from the *Bernini* statue - counter-reformation

Followed a very similar meditative process as [[René Descartes]], just simply earlier

**Process**
There is a need to be skeptical
Our false beliefs return
The evil deceiver could exist
Defeating doubt and feeling certain - a fundamental truth arises
(And a conclusion of God's existence because of this belief)
