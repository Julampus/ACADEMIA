	Uses famous Greeks as a foundation for his plays...
	"Basically fanfiction"

Teacher to [[Aristotle]], was taught by (and wrote from the perspective of) [[Socrates]] (though their relationship was likely that of a professor and student rather than a pederastic one)

Created a school lasting several centuries: *The Academy*

Constructed the allegory of the cave: of ascending to a higher state of knowledge and seeing the deceptions of the world for what it is (learning and obtaining knowledge is the process listed here)

The [[Form]] of a thing is its essential being (an extension of Socratic thought) - we are merely copies of the form of Man (or is it a part of the form of Man, taking part in the form...)

On justice: it is the philosopher kings (fully wise, imotive rulers) that ought to rule and decide how a city should be ruled
