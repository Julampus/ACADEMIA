#philosophy 

Lover of [[Hannah Arendt]], for a time, philosopher of [[Continental Philosophy]], [[Phenomenology]], and [[Existentialism]], and active member of the Nazi party
We often do not exist as an authentic self, because authenticity is the active recognition of the self -> E.g. [[Cogito]] only works once it is in the process of being thought up

In *Being and Time*, Heidegger discusses [[Phenomenology]]

Spoke of [[Being (Sein)]] as being constructive of Dasein - existing as a being-in-the-world where the subject-object relation falls apart (the quality of being *thrown in the world* - thrownness)- as well as Alethia - disclosure and manifestation of the self
Talks of *angst* as the dread of death and individuality, a constant mood of life. 
Fallenness describes Dasein's tendency to fail itself, and projection is its desire to exist in the things around us. These two characters are in conflict.
Heidegger later described the Jews as anti-Dasein in his black notebooks
